---
key: Scenario
summary: The mission a campaign opens with, or the mission number from which a music track may be picked.
---

This key means different things in its two scopes. In a campaign's section of `battle.ini` it names the [map the campaign opens with](/keys/scenario/#scope-campaign). In a music track's section of `theme.ini` it is the [campaign level number a single-player game must reach](/keys/scenario/#scope-themes) before the track can play.
