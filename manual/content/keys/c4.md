A player-controlled saboteur gets the demolitions cursor over a non-allied [`Repairable=yes`](/keys/repairable/) structure. Clicking it sends the saboteur to arm a charge, which [Arming a structure](/systems/capture/#arming-a-structure) covers; the saboteur survives it.

Over a structure that is not `Repairable=yes`, the saboteur gets the ordinary attack cursor, but it does not shoot. The attack order turns into a capture order, so the saboteur walks into the structure and is consumed with no effect. [A commando over a structure](/systems/capture/#a-commando-over-a-structure) covers deployed vehicles and force fire.

`C4=yes` also turns [`Infiltrate=yes`](/keys/infiltrate/) on after the section is read, so an `Infiltrate=no` in the same section has no effect.

A computer-owned saboteur includes structures in its target scan and switches to sabotage when its target is a `Repairable=yes` structure. [Computer-controlled engineers](/systems/capture/#computer-controlled-engineers) lists those mission changes.

A human player's saboteur does not [retaliate](/systems/target-selection/#retaliation) against a structure that damages it.

Other infantry are killed when they finish a move on a rock or water cell while not on a bridge. A saboteur is not.

The [`C4` veteran ability](/systems/veterancy/#abilities) gives a soldier the same cursor over structures, mission changes, retaliation rule and rock and water exemption, so a type that gains it at a rank acts as a saboteur without this setting. The ability does not turn `Infiltrate` on, and it does not give the demolitions cursor over a deployed vehicle.
