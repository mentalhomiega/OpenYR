`SuperAnimTwoDamaged=` names the animation that super slot two runs in place of [`SuperAnimTwo`](/keys/superanimtwo/) while the structure's health is at or below [`ConditionYellow`](/keys/conditionyellow/). [The damaged form](/systems/building-animations/#the-damaged-form) covers when the structure switches between the two forms.
