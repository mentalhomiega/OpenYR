All of a house's `FirestormWall=yes` sections rise together when the house fires its [firestorm superweapon](/systems/superweapons/#the-firestorm-defense), and drop together when the defense goes offline. [The firestorm wall](/systems/laser-fences/#the-firestorm-wall) covers what the flag does to placement, to the shape a section draws, to what a raised section destroys, and to what selling one returns.

The [power toggle](/systems/power/#switching-a-structure-off) refuses a wall section, so the player cannot switch one off to open a gap in the line.
