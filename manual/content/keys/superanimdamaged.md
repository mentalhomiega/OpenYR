`SuperAnimDamaged=` names the animation that super slot one runs in place of [`SuperAnim`](/keys/superanim/) while the structure's health is at or below [`ConditionYellow`](/keys/conditionyellow/). [The damaged form](/systems/building-animations/#the-damaged-form) covers when the structure switches between the two forms.
