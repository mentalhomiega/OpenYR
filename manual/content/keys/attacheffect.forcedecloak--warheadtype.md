---
key: AttachEffect.ForceDecloak
scope: warheadtype
label: Uncloak on hit
when_omitted:
  kind: value
  value: "no"
---

`AttachEffect.ForceDecloak=yes` uncloaks a cloaked or cloaking object each time this warhead attaches its effect to it or restarts the effect. An object that can cloak may cloak again as usual afterwards.
