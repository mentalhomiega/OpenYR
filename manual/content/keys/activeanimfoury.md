Moves the [`ActiveAnimFour`](/keys/activeanimfour/) animation this many screen pixels down from the structure's drawing point. A negative value moves it up.

[Placement and draw order](/systems/building-animations/#placement-and-draw-order) covers how the offset differs from the two draw-order biases.
