`SuperLowPowerGarrisoned=` names the animation the super low power slot is meant to run in place of [`SuperLowPower`](/keys/superlowpower/) while infantry occupy the structure. OpenTS reads the value but does not play it. [The damaged form](/systems/building-animations/#the-damaged-form) covers the slot's three names.
