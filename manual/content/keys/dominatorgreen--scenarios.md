While a [psychic dominator](/systems/superweapons/#psychic-dominator) blast lasts, `DominatorGreen` is the multiplier on the green channel of every terrain palette and of the lit house color schemes. [`DominatorRed`](/keys/dominatorred/) explains the scale.
