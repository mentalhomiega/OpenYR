---
key: AttachEffect.Cloakable
scope: aircrafttype
label: Cloak while own effect lasts
when_omitted:
  kind: value
  value: "no"
---

`AttachEffect.Cloakable=yes` lets an object of this type cloak while its [own effect](/systems/attach-effects/#an-object-types-own-effect) lasts, as a [warhead's effect](/keys/attacheffect.cloakable/#scope-warheadtype) does.
