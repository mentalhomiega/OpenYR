label: Own rate of fire multiplier
when_omitted:
  kind: value
  value: "1.0"
  note: "Reload time is unchanged."
---

`AttachEffect.ROFMultiplier` multiplies the reload time of an object of this type while its [own effect](/systems/attach-effects/#an-object-types-own-effect) lasts, in the same way as a [warhead's rate of fire multiplier](/keys/attacheffect.rofmultiplier/#scope-warheadtype).
