`ActiveAnimThreeGarrisoned=` names the animation active slot three runs in place of [`ActiveAnimThree`](/keys/activeanimthree/) while infantry occupy the structure and its health is above `ConditionYellow`. [The damaged form](/systems/building-animations/#the-damaged-form) covers the slot's three names.
