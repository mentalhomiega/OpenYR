---
key: AttachEffect.FirepowerMultiplier
scope: aircrafttype
label: Own firepower multiplier
when_omitted:
  kind: value
  value: "1.0"
  note: "Damage dealt is unchanged."
---

`AttachEffect.FirepowerMultiplier` multiplies the damage of the shots an object of this type fires while its [own effect](/systems/attach-effects/#an-object-types-own-effect) lasts, in the same way as a [warhead's firepower multiplier](/keys/attacheffect.firepowermultiplier/#scope-warheadtype).
