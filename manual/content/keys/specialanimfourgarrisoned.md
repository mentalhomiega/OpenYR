`SpecialAnimFourGarrisoned=` names the animation special slot four is meant to run in place of [`SpecialAnimFour`](/keys/specialanimfour/) while infantry occupy the structure. OpenTS reads the value but does not play it. [The damaged form](/systems/building-animations/#the-damaged-form) covers the slot's three names.
