`IdleAnimDamaged=` names the animation that the idle slot runs in place of [`IdleAnim`](/keys/idleanim/) while the structure's health is at or below [`ConditionYellow`](/keys/conditionyellow/). [The damaged form](/systems/building-animations/#the-damaged-form) covers when the structure switches between the two forms.
