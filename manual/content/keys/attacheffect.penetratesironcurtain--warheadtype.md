---
key: AttachEffect.PenetratesIronCurtain
scope: warheadtype
label: Attach through Iron Curtain
when_omitted:
  kind: value
  value: "no"
---

`AttachEffect.PenetratesIronCurtain=yes` lets this warhead attach its effect to an object protected by the Iron Curtain or a Force Shield. With `no`, a protected object gets no effect from the hit; an effect it already carries stays.
