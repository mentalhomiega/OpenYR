`SuperAnimGarrisoned=` names the animation super slot one runs in place of [`SuperAnim`](/keys/superanim/) while infantry occupy the structure and its health is above `ConditionYellow`. [The damaged form](/systems/building-animations/#the-damaged-form) covers the slot's three names.
