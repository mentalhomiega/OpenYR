---
key: AttachEffect.PenetratesIronCurtain
scope: aircrafttype
label: Attach own effect through Iron Curtain
when_omitted:
  kind: value
  value: "no"
---

`AttachEffect.PenetratesIronCurtain=yes` lets an object of this type attach its [own effect](/systems/attach-effects/#an-object-types-own-effect) while it is protected by the Iron Curtain or a Force Shield. With `no`, the effect waits until the protection ends.
