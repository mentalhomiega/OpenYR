`LowPowerDamaged=` names the animation that the low power slot runs in place of [`LowPower`](/keys/lowpower/) while the structure's health is at or below [`ConditionYellow`](/keys/conditionyellow/). [The damaged form](/systems/building-animations/#the-damaged-form) covers when the structure switches between the two forms.
