`SpecialAnimFourDamaged=` names the animation that special slot four runs in place of [`SpecialAnimFour`](/keys/specialanimfour/) while the structure's health is at or below [`ConditionYellow`](/keys/conditionyellow/). [The damaged form](/systems/building-animations/#the-damaged-form) covers when the structure switches between the two forms.
