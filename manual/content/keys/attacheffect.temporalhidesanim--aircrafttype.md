---
key: AttachEffect.TemporalHidesAnim
scope: aircrafttype
label: Hide own animation while warped
when_omitted:
  kind: value
  value: "no"
---

`AttachEffect.TemporalHidesAnim=yes` hides the animation of an object's [own effect](/systems/attach-effects/#an-object-types-own-effect) while a temporal weapon is warping it out, as it does for a [warhead's effect](/keys/attacheffect.temporalhidesanim/#scope-warheadtype).
