type: event
id: TEVENT_DESTROYED
title: Destroyed by any house
summary: Satisfied when the tagged object is killed with an attacker credited, whatever house the attacker belongs to.
caveats:
  - Any attacker counts, including an ally or another object of the same house.
  - A loss that credits no attacker satisfies nothing. Selling a structure, a change of owner such as a capture, a vehicle sinking, and an aircraft brought down by an ion storm all credit no attacker.
  - Being crushed satisfies the event, because the vehicle that runs the object over is credited. A passenger that dies with its transport is credited to whatever destroyed the transport, so it satisfies the event only when the transport's death credits an attacker.
  - Two removals are not recorded as kills and never satisfy this event. They are infantry consumed as it walks into a structure or takes over a vehicle, and infantry or an aircraft on ice that breaks. [Destroyed by anything](/mapping/events/tevent-destroyed-any/) is satisfied at both.
  - A vehicle taken over by infantry does not satisfy the event when it is later destroyed, if the tag it then holds is [transferable](/systems/capture/#the-vehicle-branch).
