## What raises the leave-map mark

The mark belongs to the team, not to its members. [Leaving the map](/systems/ai-team-execution/#leaving-the-map) lists everything that raises and lowers it. Most of those causes need nobody to leave the map:

- Giving the team a mission target in a cell outside the playable area raises the mark as soon as the target is assigned.
- Every frame, each infantry member that has been inside the playable area raises the mark, wherever it stands, unless it is in a tunnel or aboard a transport.

A team with such infantry keeps the mark for good. Only a new mission target inside the playable area lowers it, and the infantry raise it again in the same frame. Such a team satisfies the event when it is wiped out and then deleted, as the next section describes.

## When the offer is made

The event is offered only when a team carrying the mark is deleted for having no members left. [The logic turn](/systems/ai-team-execution/#the-logic-turn) deletes an empty team under **any of:**

- it holds the started mark, which a team gets the first time it reaches full strength or is flagged into action;
- in a skirmish or multiplayer game, it has existed longer than [`DissolveUnfilledTeamDelay`](/keys/dissolveunfilledteamdelay/).

Just before that team is deleted, the event is offered to every tag on the general list, not to a tag connected with the team. Each tag then looks for an empty, marked team of the type its own event names. The team being deleted is still present and can be found.

Any other empty, marked team of the named type also satisfies the test. In a campaign, a team that never started is not deleted when it empties, so such a team satisfies the event the next time any marked team is deleted.
