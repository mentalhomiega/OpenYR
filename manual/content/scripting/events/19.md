## What registers a structure

A structure registers its type in its owner's record in two cases:

- The owner places the structure after producing it.
- A house [discovers](/systems/map-visibility/#what-the-other-houses-know) the structure. The record written is always the owner's, whichever house makes the discovery.

Discovery lets the event react to structures the house never built. In a campaign, uncovering an enemy base writes that enemy house's record, one structure at a time, in the order the player finds them. Outside a campaign, every human player discovers each structure as soon as it is placed, so a structure placed during the game registers at once. A forced placement, described below, is the exception.

Discoveries made while the scenario loads register nothing. A structure within the player's view when a campaign mission starts therefore does not register, while one hidden at the start registers when the player first sees it. Outside a campaign, no structure that starts on the map registers through discovery.

A structure that [Create Building At...](/mapping/actions/taction-create-building-at/) places with its force flag set follows the same rule. In a campaign, one placed within the player's view never registers, and one placed out of view registers when the player first sees it. Outside a campaign, it never registers through discovery.

The event sees only the type registered last before its house's list is tested. When several structures of the house register in the same frame, such as when the player uncovers part of a base at once, only one of them can satisfy the event.

To test whether the house currently owns a structure of a type, use [Building exists...](/mapping/events/tevent-building-exists/).
