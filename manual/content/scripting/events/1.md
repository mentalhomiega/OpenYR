## Where the offer comes from

The event can be offered to a map cell's tag or to an object's tag. A scenario can use either or both. An object's tag also receives the offer when the object [changes owner](#changing-hands-offers-it-as-well).

A **cell's tag** receives the offer when infantry or a vehicle finishes moving into the cell, under **all of:**

- the object is not fully cloaked;
- the cell has a tag;
- **any of:**
  - the cell is not under a bridge and not under a destroyed bridge span;
  - the object is on the bridge deck.

An **object's tag** receives the offer when infantry arrives at the object it was ordered to reach. That covers:

- infantry loading into a transport or entering a structure, even when the transport then refuses it;
- infantry on a capture, area guard or patrol mission walking into the structure it is heading for, whatever kind of soldier it is. This includes an engineer capturing, restoring or damaging a structure, an engineer repairing a bridge at its repair hut, and a spy infiltrating a structure;
- infantry planting a demolition charge on a structure;
- a thief stealing a vehicle, and infantry on a capture, area guard or patrol mission reaching the vehicle it is heading for.

Each of these offers names the arriving infantry, so the event tests the infantry's owner. [Capture](/systems/capture/#walking-in) says which infantry can walk into what.

## Changing hands offers it as well

Every change of owner offers the event to the tag the object holds at that moment. That includes engineer capture, vehicle theft, the Change House actions and a team's change-house mission. When a structure is captured, the last passenger to enter it and an aircraft or vehicle docked at it change owner with it and receive their own offers. Any other passengers inside stay with the house losing the structure.

The ownership offer names the object being taken, and ownership moves only afterward. The event therefore tests the house losing the object, not the house taking it.

When infantry takes the object, its arrival offers come first and the ownership offer follows. An engineer that captures a structure makes two arrival offers, so a trigger on a persistent tag naming the engineer's house fires twice before the ownership offer. An action or a team mission makes only the ownership offer. So does a vehicle that runs over a vehicle thief heading for it, which [hands the vehicle to the thief's house](/systems/capture/#stealing-a-vehicle).

For example, a Nod structure carries a persistent tag holding two triggers, one reading "Entered by GDI" and one reading "Entered by Nod". When a GDI engineer captures the structure, the arrival offers satisfy the GDI trigger and the ownership offer satisfies the Nod trigger. A volatile tag that fires on an arrival offer is removed from the structure at once, so the ownership offer does not reach it.

When an engineer captures a structure or a `Thief=yes` soldier steals a vehicle, a [transferable](/systems/capture/#the-vehicle-branch) tag on the soldier replaces the object's tag before ownership changes. The ownership offer then goes to the soldier's tag. A soldier that takes a vehicle through the vehicle branch, such as a `VehicleThief=yes` hijacker, passes its tag on only after the ownership offer.

A tag ignores every offer made while it is still handling an earlier offer. [Change House](/mapping/actions/taction-change-house/) transfers only the objects whose tag holds the trigger running the action, so its ownership offer reaches a tag that is still handling the offer that fired the trigger, and satisfies nothing. The offer counts only when [Force Trigger](/mapping/actions/taction-force-trigger/) fired that trigger from another tag. For the same reason, [All change house](/mapping/actions/taction-all-change-house/) makes no usable offer to the objects that hold its own trigger's tag.
