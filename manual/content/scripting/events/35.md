## What the scan takes

Each frame, the beam searches for an intruder and springs the event on its structure's tag when **all of:**

- the beam is sweeping;
- the beam's structure is still in the game, is [powered on](/systems/power/#fields-fences-and-lights), and has a tag;
- an infantry unit or vehicle stands in the cell under the beam or in one of the eight cells around it;
- that infantry unit or vehicle is not allied to the structure's owner;
- it is closer to the beam than [the beam's detection radius](/keys/spotlightradius/) plus 30 leptons.

The detection radius grows as the beam swings farther from its structure. The search always covers the same nine cells, so a radius that reaches past them detects nothing farther out.

## Which beams reach the scan

[The beam's behavior](/reference/enums/spotlight-behavior/) decides whether it searches:

| Behavior | Searches |
| --- | --- |
| Sweep (`1`) | Every frame |
| Circle (`2`) | Never |
| None (`0`) | Never |
| Follow (`3`) | Not while it has a target |

A beam set to circle or none never springs the event until [Change Light Behavior](/mapping/actions/taction-change-spotlight-behavior/) switches it to another behavior.

A following beam that loses its target goes back to sweeping and searches every frame from then on. On the frame it loses the target, it searches from the point where it stopped following. From the next frame it moves along its sweep arc again.
