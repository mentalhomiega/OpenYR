---
type: mission
id: TMISSION_LOAD
title: "Load onto Transport"
summary: "Sends the team's members into a transport that is itself a member of the team, one at a time."
caveats:
  - "The transport is a team member with room for at least one more passenger. If the TaskForce lists an aircraft type that carries passengers, only an aircraft member can be the transport; otherwise only a vehicle member can. If several members qualify, the most recently joined one is used."
  - "At most one member is ordered aboard each turn, and none while the transport is in radio contact, as it is while taking a passenger aboard. A member already on its way in keeps going."
  - "The line holds the team until every member [in formation](/systems/ai-team-execution/#bringing-a-member-into-formation) and every aircraft, other than the transport, is aboard. A member still on its way into formation does not hold the team, so the line can end without it."
  - "If no member qualifies as the transport, the team moves on to the next line on the following turn. A full transport does not qualify, so the line also ends once every possible transport is full, even if members are still outside."
---
