title: "Ion storm start in..."
summary: "Schedules an ion storm that lasts the given number of game frames, unless one is already running."
caveats:
  - "The number is in game frames. The [Ion Storm start...](/mapping/actions/taction-ion-storm-start/) trigger action counts in seconds of 15 frames each, so a positive number lasts 15 times as long there."
  - "The storm breaks after the [warning countdown](/systems/ion-storms/#the-warning), which also covers a second start made during the countdown."
  - "The line does not hold the team: it runs once, whether or not it starts a storm, and the team takes the next line of its script on the following turn."
related:
  - type: action

  - type: system
    id: ion-storms
---

A length of `-1` gives a storm that never ends on its own. [Starting a storm](/systems/ion-storms/#starting-a-storm) describes what other lengths of `0` or less do.
