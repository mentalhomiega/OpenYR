id: TMISSION_PLAY_MUSIC
title: "Play music..."
summary: "Queues the named music track, which starts once the current track has faded out, or at once with a crossfade."
caveats:
  - "The track does not start at once. Any track already playing fades out first, and the queued track starts after the fade, or during it with a crossfade."
  - "Only one track can wait at a time. While a queued track is still waiting, the line is ignored."
  - "The line runs once, and the team moves to its next line on the following turn."
related:
  - type: action
    id: TACTION_PLAY_MUSIC
  - type: system
    id: music
---

The track is queued the same way as by the [Play music theme...](/mapping/actions/taction-play-music/) trigger action, whose page lists the cases in which nothing is queued. [Changing tracks](/systems/music/#changing-tracks) describes the fade.
