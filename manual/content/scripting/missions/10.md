## Picking what to follow

Every turn, the team follows the vehicle or infantry nearest its most recently joined member that meets **all of:**

- it is alive and out of [limbo](/glossary/#limbo);
- its house is allied to the team's house;
- its house is not the team's own house;
- it is not a member of this team.

The followed object becomes [the team's center](/systems/ai-team-execution/#the-teams-center). If that object is inside a tunnel, the center is the tunnel's exit cell instead, and the team gathers at the exit.

## Following and joining in

The team walks toward the followed object, or toward the tunnel's exit cell while that object is in a tunnel, on the same terms as an ordinary [move](/systems/ai-team-execution/#moving). Arriving there ends the line, even when the object is still in the tunnel.

The team also joins the followed object's fights. Each turn, every member [in formation](/systems/ai-team-execution/#bringing-a-member-into-formation), and every aircraft, is handled as follows:

- A member that holds a target other than the followed object's target drops it. When the followed object has no target, every member that holds one drops it.
- When the followed object has a target, a member that is armed, standing still and on the Guard mission is put on the Attack mission against that target.

While the followed object is inside a tunnel, members neither drop their targets nor take its target.
