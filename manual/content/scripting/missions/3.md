title: "Move to waypoint..."
summary: "Sends the team to a waypoint, or to a nearby cell when the team's leading member cannot enter the waypoint's cell."
caveats:
  - "A nearby cell is looked for only when the waypoint is inside the playable area. A move to a waypoint outside it keeps the waypoint's cell as the destination, whether or not the leading member can enter it. This is how a team is sent off the map."
  - "The nearby cell is chosen for the leading member's `SpeedType` alone, not for the slowest or largest member. It is checked for being clear, but not for being reachable from where the team stands."
  - "If no nearby cell is found, the team has no destination and stays on this line until a regroup rewinds its Script, which only a `Reinforce=yes` team can do."
  - "The team moves to its next line only once it arrives. A member that cannot get within `Stray` of the destination keeps the whole team on this line, unless the TeamType sets `Aggressive=yes` and that member holds a target."

The destination is chosen once, when the team reaches the line. The waypoint's cell is used unless the team's leading member cannot enter it at that moment, for example because the ground is impassable for it or something stands there. In that case the destination is one of the nearest clear cells inside the playable area.

The leading member is the most recently joined member that is alive, out of [limbo](/glossary/#limbo), and either [in formation](/systems/ai-team-execution/#bringing-a-member-into-formation) or an aircraft. When no member qualifies, it is the most recently joined member.

[Moving](/systems/ai-team-execution/#moving) describes how members are sent to the destination and when the team counts as arrived. [Leaving the map](/systems/ai-team-execution/#leaving-the-map) covers a team sent outside the playable area.
