---
type: mission
id: TMISSION_FLASH
title: "Flash"
summary: "Flashes every member for the given number of frames."
caveats:
  - "The number is a count of game frames, so `15` flashes the members for one second at 15 frames a second. A negative number is read as a very large count, so the members keep flashing."
  - "Every member flashes, whether or not it is in formation. The flash is set on the turn the line is reached and not renewed, so a member that joins the team afterward does not flash."
  - "The line does not hold the team: it runs on the turn it is reached and the team takes the next line on the following turn."
---
