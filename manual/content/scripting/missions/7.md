  - "In a multiplayer game, each machine flags the player at that machine, so every player wins. An observer is flagged too, but sees no caption and hears no announcement."
  - "The line does not hold the team: it runs once and the team takes the next line of its script on the following turn."

The line has the same effect as a [Winner is...](/mapping/actions/taction-win/) trigger action that names the player's house. That page covers the pause before the mission ends, the caption and announcement, and when a win is ignored.
