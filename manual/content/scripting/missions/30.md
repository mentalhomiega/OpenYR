type: mission
id: TMISSION_FIRE_SALE
title: "Fire sale"
summary: "Puts the house the TeamType names into its endgame state, in which its AI keeps selling its structures and sending its other objects to hunt."
caveats:
  - "The house is the one the TeamType's `House` names. That need not be the house that owns the team, because a team raised by an AI trigger or by a Change team line can belong to another house."
  - "Only a computer-controlled house acts on the endgame state. For a house a human plays, or one whose country sets `MultiplayPassive=yes`, the line has no effect."
  - "The line changes nothing when the TeamType names no house, or names a country or start position that no house in the scenario holds."
  - "The house never leaves the endgame state."
  - "The line runs once, and the team moves to its next line on the following turn."
related:
  - type: key
    id: House
  - type: action
    id: TACTION_FIRE_SALE
---

The line sets the same state as the [Fire Sale...](/mapping/actions/taction-fire-sale/) trigger action, whose page describes when the selling starts and what the house does on each AI pass.
