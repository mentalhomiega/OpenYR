## What the team is aimed at

Each turn until it has a target, the team looks at the waypoint's cell. The first object found there becomes the target for the rest of the line, including a vehicle or infantry that moves onto the cell after the line starts. The target stays that object if it moves off the cell, and the team does not look at the cell again.

## Walking in rather than shooting

Each member in formation, and each aircraft, is ordered against the target. An infantry member whose type is `Infiltrate=yes` is put on the capture mission against it. Every other member is put on the Attack mission, so only members without `Infiltrate=yes` are ordered to fire at the target.

A soldier on the capture mission walks to the target only in these cases:

- an [`Engineer=yes`](/keys/engineer/#scope-infantrytype) or [`C4=yes`](/keys/c4/) soldier, or one that has gained the C4 veteran ability, walks into any structure;
- any other `Infiltrate=yes` soldier, such as an [`Agent=yes`](/keys/agent/) spy, walks in only when it is unarmed and the structure is [`Capturable=yes`](/keys/capturable/).

A soldier outside those cases is not sent to the target, and neither is any `Infiltrate=yes` soldier when the target is not a structure.

`Engineer=yes` and `C4=yes` both turn `Infiltrate=yes` on, so an engineer or demolition soldier is ordered in without the setting being written. A spy is ordered in only when its section sets `Infiltrate=yes`; otherwise it is put on the Attack mission like the rest of the team.

[Capture](/systems/capture/#walking-in) covers what a soldier does when it walks in. In short, an `Engineer=yes` soldier captures, repairs or damages the structure, an `Agent=yes` soldier spies on it, and [any other soldier is consumed](/systems/capture/#the-soldier-is-consumed).
