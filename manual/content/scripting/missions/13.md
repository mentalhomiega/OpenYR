---
type: mission
id: TMISSION_IDLE_ANIM
title: "Idle Anim..."
summary: "Has every infantry member play the numbered idle animation."
valid_values:
  - "`1` plays the `Idle2` entry of the soldier's [`Sequence`](/keys/sequence/) section."
  - "Any other number plays the `Idle1` entry, so `0` and `7` have the same effect."
caveats:
  - "Only infantry have idle animations. The line does nothing to vehicles and aircraft."
  - "A soldier does not play the animation when its sequence section has no entry for it, when it is already playing that animation, or when its current action cannot be interrupted."
  - "The line does not hold the team. It runs once, and the team runs the next line of its Script on the following turn."
---
