title: "Talk Bubble"
summary: "Puts a talk bubble over one member of the team."
caveats:
  - "The bubble goes over the member that joined the team most recently, whether or not it is in formation."
  - "`0` takes down the bubble showing, even when it is over an object outside the team."
  - "The line does not hold the team: it runs on the turn it is reached and the team takes the next line on the following turn."
---
