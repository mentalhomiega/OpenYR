id: TMISSION_GATHER_AT_ENEMY
title: "Gather at enemy base"
summary: "Moves the team to a spot outside its enemy's base, on the side facing its own base."
caveats:
  - "The spot lies [`AISafeDistance`](/keys/aisafedistance/) cells from the center of the base of the house the team's house treats as its enemy, in the direction of the team's own base center. With no base of its own, the team's leader stands in for it."
  - "The spot is moved to the nearest clear 3x3 area that the leader's `SpeedType` can stand in. If none is found, the line is skipped."
  - "A house with no enemy, or whose enemy has no base, skips the line."
  - "The leader is the member with the highest [`LeadershipRating`](/keys/leadershiprating/) among those that are alive, out of [limbo](/glossary/#limbo), and [in formation](/systems/ai-team-execution/#bringing-a-member-into-formation) or an aircraft. A tie goes to the most recently joined of them, and when no member qualifies the most recently joined member leads."
  - "The team takes the next line once it has [arrived](/systems/ai-team-execution/#moving): every member is within [`Stray`](/keys/stray/) of the spot, or three times that for an aircraft, and has stopped."
