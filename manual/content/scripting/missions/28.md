Each member's cell is emptied the same way as the waypoint's cell is by the [Reduce Tiberium At...](/mapping/actions/taction-reduce-tiberium/) trigger action, whose page explains why the whole cell is emptied. Neighboring Tiberium can then spread back under the usual [spread rules](/systems/tiberium/#spread).
