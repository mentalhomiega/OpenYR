## Gathering comes first

Only members [in formation](/systems/ai-team-execution/#bringing-a-member-into-formation), and aircraft, take the named state. A member still on its way into formation is sent to [the team's center](/systems/ai-team-execution/#the-teams-center) first.

A member in formation that holds neither a target nor a destination, and stands farther than twice [`Stray`](/keys/stray/) from the center, is sent back to the center on the Move mission. It takes the named state on a later turn, once it has arrived. When the named state is Area Guard, no member is sent back, and members take the state wherever they stand.

## Which members take the state

Each remaining member takes the named state, with its target and destination cleared, unless **any of:**

- it holds a target or a destination;
- it is already on the named state;
- the named state is Guard and the member is on the Unload mission.

A member passed over for holding a target or a destination takes the state on the first turn it holds neither, provided it is within twice `Stray` of the center or the state is Area Guard.
