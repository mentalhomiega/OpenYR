Each infantry member's fear is set to `255`. Fear falls by one point per logic frame, and a prone soldier stands up once its fear drops below `10`, about 245 frames later. The [Unpanic](/mapping/missions/tmission-unpanic/) line clears the fear at once, but a soldier that is already prone stays down, as that line's page describes. [`Fearless`](/keys/fearless/) describes the fear scale.

A frightened soldier owned by a computer house drops prone on its next turn. One owned by a human player drops prone only once it is standing still with no destination. Two kinds of soldier respond differently:

- a [`Fraidycat=yes`](/keys/fraidycat/) soldier also scatters whenever it stands still with no destination;
- a [`Doggie=yes`](/keys/doggie/#fear) dog never drops prone. It lies down on Tiberium, or walks to Tiberium nearby, as its key page describes.
