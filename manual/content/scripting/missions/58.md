  - "The line picks once, on the turn it starts."
  - "The destination is chosen as for Moveto enemy building. The team takes the next line on the turn after the pick finds no structure or no clear cell near it, and otherwise once it arrives."
  - "Keep the building type position inside the list. The line uses it without a range check."
