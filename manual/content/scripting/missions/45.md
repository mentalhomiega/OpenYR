---
type: mission
id: TMISSION_LOAD_TRUCK
title: "Truck load"
summary: "Turns every TRUCKA vehicle in the team into a TRUCKB, so the truck looks loaded without carrying any cargo."
caveats:
  - "The two type IDs are fixed as `TRUCKA` for the empty truck and `TRUCKB` for the loaded one. No other pair can be used."
  - "From then on the truck takes its settings, such as its artwork, from `TRUCKB`. How it moves is still set by the `Locomotor` of `TRUCKA`, and its current health is kept as the same number of hit points."
  - "Members of every other type are left unchanged."
  - "Keep `TRUCKB` registered as a vehicle type. If a team holding a `TRUCKA` reaches this line without one, the truck is given an invalid type and the game can crash."
  - "The line does not hold the team: it runs once and the team takes the next line of its script on the following turn."
related:

    id: TMISSION_UNLOAD_TRUCK
---
