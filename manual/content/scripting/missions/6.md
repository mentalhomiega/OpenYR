---
type: mission
id: TMISSION_LOOP
title: 'Jump to line #'
summary: A script mission that continues the team's Script from a one-based line number.
valid_values:
  - The number of a line in the Script, counting the Script's lines in slot order from `1`. The line in the lowest slot, normally `0=`, is line `1`, and an unused slot number is not counted.
caveats:
  - The team takes the target line on the turn after the jump line runs.
  - A backward jump makes a loop with no limit on the number of passes.
  - "`0`, a negative number, or a number past the last line moves the team off the end of its Script. The team is deleted on the following turn, as if it had finished its Script."
related: []
---

[Scripts](/mapping/scripts/) covers the slots a Script's lines are written in.

:::caution[Give a loop a way out]
A team in a loop repeats it until the team is removed. If the team should stop looping, put a [Change script...](/mapping/missions/tmission-script/) or [Change team...](/mapping/missions/tmission-teamchange/) line inside the loop, or disband the team with a [Destroy Team...](/mapping/actions/taction-destroy-team/) trigger action.
:::
