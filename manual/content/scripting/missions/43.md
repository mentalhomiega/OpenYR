id: TMISSION_FULLY_LOADED
title: "Wait till fully loaded"
summary: "Holds the team until every member that carries passengers has as many aboard as its `Passengers` value."
caveats:
  - "A member that cannot carry passengers, such as an infantryman, counts as full."
  - "The line compares the number of passengers aboard with the transport's [`Passengers`](/keys/passengers/) value and ignores the space each passenger takes. A transport whose passengers have a [`Size`](/keys/size/) above 1 can be full and still hold the team on this line forever."
  - "The line orders nobody aboard. Load the members with another line first, such as [Load onto Transport](/mapping/missions/tmission-load/). A transport that never fills holds the team on this line."
