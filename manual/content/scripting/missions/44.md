caveats:
  - "The two vehicle types are fixed in the engine: a member of type `TRUCKB`, the loaded truck, becomes type `TRUCKA`, the empty one. No other pair can be used, and members of every other type are passed over."
  - "The member takes on everything its new type sets, including its artwork. Its current health is kept."
  - "Keep a `TRUCKA` vehicle type defined while any team can run this line on a `TRUCKB`. Without one, the truck is given an invalid type and the game can crash."
  - "The line does not hold the team: it runs once and the team takes the next line of its script on the following turn."
