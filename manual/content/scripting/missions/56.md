valid_values:
  - "One number that holds both parameters, as for [Attack enemy building](/mapping/missions/tmission-attack-building-with-property/): the target property times 65536, plus the building type's position in the registered building type list, counted from `0`."
caveats:
  - "The house is that of the team's leader, the member with the highest [`LeadershipRating`](/keys/leadershiprating/) among those that are alive, out of [limbo](/glossary/#limbo), and [in formation](/systems/ai-team-execution/#bringing-a-member-into-formation) or an aircraft. When no member qualifies, the most recently joined member leads."
  - "The house needs a weapon with `Type=ChronoSphere` and one with `Type=ChronoWarp`. The ChronoSphere weapon must be ready and the house must have full power. Then the line picks the structure as Attack enemy building does, fires the ChronoSphere weapon at the team's center and the ChronoWarp weapon at the structure, and takes the structure as the team's mission target. The team takes the next line on that turn, whether or not it found a structure."
  - "A house without both weapons skips the line. So does one whose ChronoSphere weapon is not ready and is less than [`AIMinorSuperReadyPercent`](/keys/aiminorsuperreadypercent/) charged. A house whose weapon is further along waits on this line until it can fire."
