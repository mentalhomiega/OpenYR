---
type: mission
id: TMISSION_MOVECELL
title: "Move to Cell..."
summary: "Sends the team to a map cell given by its number."
valid_values:
  - "A cell number in decimal, `X + Y * 1000`. The cell at X 50, Y 60 is `60050`."
caveats:
  - "In a scenario whose [`NewINIFormat`](/keys/newiniformat/) is below `4` or absent, write the number as `X + Y * 128` instead. The game converts it when the script is read. The same conversion applies to this line in `ai.ini` scripts whenever the map being played is in the older format, so an `ai.ini` value names a different cell on such a map."
  - "A number that names a cell outside the playfield gives the team no destination. The team then stays on this line until a [regroup](/systems/ai-team-execution/#every-regroup-rewinds-the-script) rewinds its script."
  - "The cell is not tested for being reachable or for having room to stand."
  - "The team takes the next line once it has [arrived](/systems/ai-team-execution/#moving): every member is within [`Stray`](/keys/stray/) of the cell, or three times that for an aircraft, and has stopped. One member that cannot get there holds the team on this line."
related:

    id: TMISSION_MOVE
