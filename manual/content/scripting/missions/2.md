---
type: mission
id: TMISSION_BERZERK
title: Go Berzerk
summary: Sends every infantry member of the team berserk for the rest of the scenario.
caveats:
  - Only infantry go berserk, so vehicles and aircraft in the team are unaffected. Any infantry type qualifies, whether or not it is a `Cyborg=yes` type.
  - "`BerzerkAllowed` does not affect this line. It controls only cyborgs that go berserk from damage."
  - The line gives no member a new mission. A member whose current mission looks for no targets keeps doing what it was doing.
  - The line runs once, and the team moves to its next line on the following turn.
related:
  - type: system
    id: ai-team-execution
  - type: action
    id: TACTION_GO_BERZERK
---

A berserk soldier attacks allies as well as enemies, and nothing ends the state. The [Go Berzerk](/mapping/actions/taction-go-berzerk/) trigger action has the same effect on infantry chosen by their tag, and its page describes what being berserk changes.
