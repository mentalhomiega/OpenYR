---
type: mission
id: TMISSION_ATTACK_BUILDING_WITH_PROPERTY
title: "Attack enemy building"
summary: "Picks the structure of the named type that best fits the chosen property and turns the team onto it."
valid_values:
  - "One number that holds both parameters: the target property times 65536, plus the building type's position in the registered building type list, counted from `0`. `131073` is property `2`, nearest, with building type `1`."
caveats:
  - "A structure owned by the house's declared enemy wins over every other candidate, however the property ranks them."
  - "Any other candidate must belong to a house that is not allied with the team's house. A neutral or civilian house can qualify, but the team's house and its allies never do."
  - "`OnlyTargetHouseEnemy=yes` on the TeamType limits the choice to the declared enemy's structures, so the line finds nothing when the declared enemy has none."
  - "Nearest and farthest are measured from the member that joined the team most recently, not from the team's center."
  - "When the chosen structure is destroyed, the line picks again."
  - "The team takes the next line on the turn after a pick finds no structure, or when no member in formation and no aircraft can still attack. An aircraft with a weapon and no ammunition cannot attack."
  - "Keep the building type position inside the list. The line uses it without a range check, and a position at or beyond the end of the list reads past it."
related:
  - type: mission
    id: TMISSION_MOVETO_BUILDING_WITH_PROPERTY
---

Building types are numbered in the order they are registered, which [Rules registration lists](/formats/rules-registries/) describes. [`OnlyTargetHouseEnemy`](/keys/onlytargethouseenemy/) covers the declared enemy, including a house that has none.
