title: "Unpanic"
summary: "Removes all fear from every infantry member of the team."
caveats:
  - "Only infantry have a fear state, so vehicles and aircraft are unaffected. An infantry whose type sets [`Fearless=yes`](/keys/fearless/) keeps any fear it has."
  - "The line removes fear from any cause, such as taking fire, as well as the fear [Panic](/mapping/missions/tmission-panic/) gives."
  - "Removing fear does not make a prone infantry member stand up. It stays down until something else raises it, such as new fear wearing off."
  - "The line does not hold the team: it runs once and the team takes the next line of its script on the following turn."
