type: mission
id: TMISSION_IRON_CURTAIN_ME
title: "Iron Curtain me"
summary: "Fires one of the house's Iron Curtains on the team."
valid_values:
  - "The [`SW.Group`](/keys/sw.group/) number of the Iron Curtain to fire. `0` picks the weapons whose group is `0`, which is every weapon that sets no group. This parameter is an Ares feature."
caveats:
  - "The house is that of the member with the highest [`LeadershipRating`](/keys/leadershiprating/) among those that are alive, out of [limbo](/glossary/#limbo), and [in formation](/systems/ai-team-execution/#bringing-a-member-into-formation) or an aircraft. When no member qualifies, the most recently joined member is used."
  - "A weapon is a candidate when its `SW.Group` equals the line's parameter and it is a `Type=IronCurtain` weapon that does not set another [`SW.AITargeting`](/keys/sw.aitargeting/) mode, or a weapon of any other type that sets `SW.AITargeting=IronCurtain`."
  - "The first candidate that is ready is fired at the team's center, provided the house has at least as much power as it drains. The team then takes the next line. [Iron curtain](/systems/superweapons/#iron-curtain) covers what the shot does."
  - "A house with no candidate skips the line. So does one whose candidates are all not ready and less than [`AIMinorSuperReadyPercent`](/keys/aiminorsuperreadypercent/) charged. A house with a candidate further along waits on this line until it can fire."
