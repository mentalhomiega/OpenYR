---
type: mission
id: TMISSION_SUCCESS
title: Success
summary: Marks the team as successful, which counts only when the team is removed.
caveats:
  - The line changes nothing about the team, its members or their orders. The team takes the next line on the following turn.
  - Nothing clears the mark, so a team that has passed this line once counts as successful however it ends.
  - No other line or event sets the mark. A team removed before it reaches this line records a failure.
  - The outcome is recorded against every AI trigger whose first TeamType is the team's TeamType, whatever created the team. A team created by a trigger action changes those records too.
related:
  - type: system
    id: ai-team-production
  - type: system
    id: ai-team-execution
---

Nothing reads the mark while the team exists. [The track record](/systems/ai-team-production/#the-track-record) covers what happens when the team is removed: which AI triggers record the outcome and how their weights change.
