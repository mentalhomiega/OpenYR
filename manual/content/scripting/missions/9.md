---
type: mission
id: TMISSION_DEPLOY
title: "Deploy"
summary: "Orders every vehicle in the team that deploys into a structure to deploy, first moving allied units off a blocked site."
caveats:
  - "Only vehicles in formation whose type sets `DeploysInto` are ordered to deploy into a structure, and the line holds the team until they have. A vehicle with [`IsSimpleDeployer=yes`](/keys/issimpledeployer/#scope-unittype) and a soldier with [`Deployer=yes`](/keys/deployer/#scope-infantrytype) that are in formation are also told to deploy, but they never hold the team."
  - "Where the structure cannot be placed at the vehicle's position, allied infantry and vehicles standing on the site are told to move off, and the vehicle tries again on a later turn. Nothing else is cleared: a terrain object, a wall, a structure, or a unit of a house the team's house is not allied with keeps the site blocked."
  - "The line holds the team while any member in formation still has a `DeploysInto` type. A vehicle that can never deploy where it stands holds the team on this line indefinitely."
---

[`DeploysInto`](/keys/deploysinto/) names the structure a vehicle becomes. [Bringing a member into formation](/systems/ai-team-execution/#bringing-a-member-into-formation) covers when a member counts as in formation.
