id: TMISSION_BEGIN_PRODUCTION
title: "Begin production"
summary: "Marks the house the TeamType names as started, so that its factories begin building."
caveats:
  - "The house is the one the TeamType's `House` names. That need not be the house that owns the team, because a team raised by an AI trigger or by a Change team line can belong to another house."
  - "The line changes nothing when the TeamType names no house, or names a country or start position that no house in the scenario holds."
  - "Nothing clears the started mark."
  - "The line runs once, and the team moves to its next line on the following turn."
related:
  - type: key
    id: House
  - type: key
    id: Production
  - type: action
    id: TACTION_BEGIN_PRODUCTION
---

The line sets the same started mark as the [Production Begins...](/mapping/actions/taction-begin-production/) trigger action. [`Production`](/keys/production/) explains the mark, and the action's page lists the houses for which setting it changes nothing.
