---
type: mission
id: TMISSION_ATTACK
title: "Attack..."
summary: "Turns the whole team onto a target of the chosen category, chosen from one member's position."
caveats:
  - "The team picks a target whenever it has no [mission target](/systems/ai-team-execution/#the-teams-two-targets). Once a target is destroyed, the team picks the next one of the same category."
  - "The team's leader picks the target, searching from where it stands. The leader is the member with the highest [`LeadershipRating`](/keys/leadershiprating/) among those that are alive, out of [limbo](/glossary/#limbo), and [in formation](/systems/ai-team-execution/#bringing-a-member-into-formation) or an aircraft. A tie goes to the most recently joined of them, and when no member qualifies the most recently joined member leads. The leader can be unarmed even when the team has armed members."
  - "[`OnlyTargetHouseEnemy=yes`](/keys/onlytargethouseenemy/) on the TeamType limits the search to objects of [the house's declared enemy](/systems/base-attacked/#anger-and-the-declared-enemy). No other TeamType setting narrows it."
  - "Every eighth game frame the leader checks whether its weapon can ever be aimed at the team's target, for example because the weapon cannot hit that kind of object. A leader that cannot ends the line on that frame."
  - "A member of a [`Droppod=yes`](/keys/droppod/#scope-teamtype) team that is still off the map, above it on its way down, counts as able to attack, so the line does not end while the pod members are landing."
  - "The team moves on to the next line of its Script on the first turn that a pick finds no target of the category. It also moves on when every member uses ammunition and none has any left, or when no member in formation, and no aircraft member, can attack. Only an armed aircraft with no ammunition counts as unable to attack."
