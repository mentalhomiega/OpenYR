---
type: mission
id: TMISSION_CHANGE_HOUSE
title: "Change house..."
summary: "Transfers every member to the named house, as though each had been captured."
valid_values:
  - "A house by number, either a country or a spawn house. A country selects the first house in the scenario that plays it. A spawn house selects the house at that start position."
caveats:
  - "The members stay on the team. The team keeps its original house and runs the rest of its Script with members that another house now owns."
  - "A member already owned by the named house is unaffected. The line changes nothing when it names a country no house in the scenario plays, or a start position no house holds."
  - "The line runs once, and the team moves to its next line on the following turn."
related:
  - type: system
    id: capture
  - type: action
    id: TACTION_CHANGE_HOUSE
---

Each member changes owner the way a captured object does, and infantry members are included. [What changes hands](/systems/capture/#what-changes-hands) lists the effects: the member's entered trigger springs, the change is recorded as a kill, the new owner gains score, and the member's target and destination are cleared. A member on the map then returns to its idle behavior. A vehicle that another vehicle is following takes its follower to the new house as well, even when the follower is not on the team.

A later [Change team](/mapping/missions/tmission-teamchange/) line leaves the transferred members behind, because their house no longer matches the team's.
