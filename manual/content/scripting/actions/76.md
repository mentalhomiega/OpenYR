---
type: action
id: TACTION_SET_AI_TRIGGER_TEAM_RATIO
title: Ratio of AI trigger teams...
summary: Sets the percent chance that the trigger's house draws from its AI triggers when its team countdown expires.
valid_values:
  - A percentage from `1` to `100`. The house draws from its AI triggers only when a roll from `1` to `100` comes out at or below it.
caveats:
  - The number is stored without a range check. `0` or less never passes the roll, and anything above `100` always passes.
  - A failed roll raises no team of any kind and restarts the countdown. A lower number makes the house raise teams less often without changing which teams it raises.
  - The action changes the house that owns the trigger. The line names no house.
  - The house's AI-trigger switch must also be on, and it starts off. The AI triggers begin action turns it on.
