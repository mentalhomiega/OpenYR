id: TACTION_ION_CANNON
title: Ion-cannon strike...
summary: Sets off an ion cannon blast at the waypoint without using a superweapon.
caveats:
  - No superweapon, uplink, charge or owning house is involved, and nothing is announced.
  - The blast is the one a `Type=IonCannon` superweapon delivers, with the same animations, damage, warhead and shockwave. [Ion cannon](/systems/superweapons/#ion-cannon) describes it.
  - The blast lands at ground level. Where a bridge spans the waypoint's cell, the bridge deck is hit as well.
  - The blast credits no attacker, so its kills earn no score and no veterancy and make no house angry. Objects it hits do not spring [Attacked by any house](/mapping/events/tevent-attacked/) or [Destroyed by any house](/mapping/events/tevent-destroyed/).
