---
type: action
id: TACTION_SET_AMBIENT_LIGHT
title: Set ambient light...
summary: Sets the scenario's ambient light level, in hundredths of full daylight, and fades the map toward it.
valid_values:
  - A light level in hundredths, where `100` is full daylight. The map's `Ambient` key gives the level as a fraction instead, so `1` here is one hundredth of daylight.
caveats:
  - The light moves toward the new level one step at a time, at the pace [`AmbientChangeStep`](/keys/ambientchangestep/) and [`AmbientChangeRate`](/keys/ambientchangerate/) set. A fade in progress turns toward the new level without finishing first.
  - During an ion storm the new level is stored, but the light keeps the storm's level. The map fades to the new level when the storm ends.
  - Setting the level the light already has changes nothing on screen and springs no ambient light event. Those events are tested only after a fade step.
