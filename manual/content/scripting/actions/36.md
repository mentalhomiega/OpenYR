---
type: action
id: TACTION_ALL_CHANGE_HOUSE
title: All change house
summary: Hands everything the trigger's house owns to the house named.
valid_values:
  - The house that receives everything, selected by its country or by a [spawn house](/formats/scenario-objects/#spawn-houses). A country selects the first house of that country.
caveats:
  - The house that loses everything is the one that owns the trigger. Give the trigger to the side that defects, and name the side that gains its forces.
  - A country no house plays, or a spawn house nobody holds, leaves the action doing nothing.
  - Every infantry, vehicle, aircraft and structure the house owns changes hands, wherever it is and whatever state it is in. That includes passengers, cargo, objects inside a factory, and objects destroyed earlier in the same frame that are still waiting to be removed.
related:
  - type: action
    id: TACTION_CHANGE_HOUSE
  - type: system
    id: capture
  - type: event
    id: TEVENT_PLAYER_ENTERED
---

Each object changes owner through the same steps as a mind control transfer. [Taking an object over](/systems/mind-control/#taking-an-object-over) lists them, and [a structure's bookkeeping](/systems/capture/#what-changes-hands) lists what a structure brings with it.

Two of those steps matter for a whole side at once:

- Each object is recorded as a kill for the receiving house. It adds its cost to that house's score and one to that house's count of structures or units killed.
- The objects' tags are not offered [`Entered by...`](/mapping/events/tevent-player-entered/). An infantry, aircraft or structure still offers both forms of [`Destroyed by anything`](/mapping/events/tevent-destroyed-any/) as it changes hands, and the offers are skipped for one already destroyed earlier in the same frame. Vehicles offer none.

[Change House](/mapping/actions/taction-change-house/) makes the same transfer for the objects whose tag holds this trigger, and only those that are on the map.
