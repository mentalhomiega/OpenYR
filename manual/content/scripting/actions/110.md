---
type: action
id: TACTION_HOUSE_DESTROY_ALL
title: Destroy all of...
summary: Destroys everything the given house owns and marks the house defeated.
caveats:
  - A house nobody plays, or a spawn house nobody holds, leaves the action doing nothing.
  - When several houses play the named country, only one of them is destroyed. Name a spawn house to choose a particular player.
  - The house is defeated the way a skirmish or multiplayer house is, in a campaign too. When it is the player's own house, the player is left watching the map.
  - Every player is told of the defeat. A house of a [`MultiplayPassive=yes`](/keys/multiplaypassive/) country is announced only to the player who plays it.
  - The game ends when the defeat leaves only one side still playing. In a match where at least one person plays, it also ends when no person is left playing.
  - When the defeat ends the game, the outcome takes effect after the pause set by [`SavourDelay`](/keys/savourdelay/). A player who is still playing wins, even when the destroyed house was their last opponent, unless that player was already flagged to lose. The player whose house was destroyed loses, and so does a player whose house was defeated earlier.
related:
  - type: action
    id: TACTION_LOSE
  - type: format
    id: scenario-objects
---

Every object the house still owns is destroyed, whatever its kind, in the order the game keeps its objects. Each takes one hit of its remaining strength from [`C4Warhead`](/keys/c4warhead/), and that hit goes through the warhead's `Verses` table like any other damage. An object that survives its one hit is not hit again by this action. Passengers are hit in the same pass.

Mind control changes what this action takes. A unit this house lost to another house's mind control is spared. A unit this house holds by mind control belongs to the house it was taken from for this action, so it is not destroyed either. Both kinds keep their owner, and [the harvester truce](/keys/harvesterimmune/) can still remove the vehicles a defeated house owns.

[When the match ends](/systems/observers/#when-the-match-ends) gives the full rule for ending the game, and [the whole map](/systems/observers/#the-whole-map) describes what a defeated player sees. [Spawn houses](/formats/scenario-objects/#spawn-houses) lists the house numbers that name a start position in place of a country.
