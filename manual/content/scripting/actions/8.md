---
type: action
id: TACTION_DZ
title: Drop Zone Flare (waypoint)...
summary: Places a drop zone flare at the waypoint, which reveals the ground around it to the player.
valid_values:
  - A scenario waypoint, given as its label at the end of the line. An omitted label means waypoint `A`.
caveats:
  - The flare only marks the spot. It deals no damage, and it neither brings reinforcements nor tells a reinforcement action where to deliver them.
  - The flare stands on the ground. On a cell under a bridge, it stands at the height of the bridge deck. A cell whose bridge has been destroyed still raises the flare to deck height, where it hangs above the ground.
  - The flare is the animation [`DropZoneAnim`](/keys/dropzoneanim/) names. That key page describes how the flare reveals the ground around it and how a structure in its cell removes it. With `DropZoneAnim` unset, this action crashes the game.
related:
  - type: key
    id: DropZoneAnim
  - type: key
    id: DropZoneRadius
  - type: system
    id: map-visibility
---
