---
type: action
id: TACTION_FULL_SPECIAL
title: Add repeating special weapon...
summary: Gives the trigger's house a superweapon that recharges for the rest of the mission and needs no structure behind it.
valid_values:
  - The position of a superweapon in `[SuperWeaponTypes]`, counting the first section listed as `0`. A position past the end of that list can crash the game.
caveats:
  - The weapon goes to the house that owns the trigger.
  - No voice announces the grant. Later voices follow the usual [charging rules](/systems/superweapons/#charging).
  - The weapon is freed from structures even when the house already holds it. A weapon a structure granted is then kept after that structure is sold or destroyed. A [one-time copy](/systems/superweapons/#granted-outright) the house has not fired yet stays one-time and is removed when it fires.
  - A weapon that is suspended when the action runs, such as one whose structure is switched off, [stays suspended](/systems/superweapons/#granted-outright) for the rest of the game.
related:
  - type: system
    id: superweapons
  - type: action
    id: TACTION_1_SPECIAL
  - type: system
    id: sidebar
---
