---
type: action
id: TACTION_STOP_AI_TRIGGERS
title: AI triggers stop...
summary: Turns off the AI-trigger switch of the house named, so that house raises no further teams on its own.
valid_values:
  - A live house selected by a [house number](/formats/scenario-objects/#spawn-houses).
caveats:
  - AI triggers are the only source of the teams a house raises on its own, so the house raises none while the switch is off.
  - Teams the house already has are unaffected. They keep their members, go on running their scripts, and go on recruiting if they are short.
  - The AI triggers begin action turns the switch back on. In a campaign game nothing else does.
  - A house nobody plays, or a spawn house nobody holds, leaves the action doing nothing. The trigger's other actions still run.
related:

    id: TACTION_BEGIN_AI_TRIGGERS

    id: TACTION_SET_AI_TRIGGER_TEAM_RATIO
  - type: system
    id: ai-team-production
  - type: system
    id: ai-team-execution
