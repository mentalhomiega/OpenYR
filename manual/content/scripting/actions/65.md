---
type: action
id: TACTION_LIGHT_MEDIUM
title: Light flash (medium) at...
summary: Briefly lights the ground around a waypoint with the medium flash, and harms nothing there.
valid_values:
  - A scenario waypoint, written as its number in the fourth field of the line. Waypoint `A` is `0`. The action ignores the waypoint label at the end of the line.
caveats:
  - No flash appears unless the warhead that `C4Warhead` names sets `Bright=yes`.
  - Nothing is damaged, moved or destroyed. The light is the whole effect.
  - The medium flash is about a fifth wider than the small flash and two fifths the width of the large flash, so the three sizes are not evenly spaced.
  - The flash brightens and fades within about ten game frames. It is not drawn while fog of war covers the waypoint.
related:
  - type: key
    id: Bright
    scope: warheadtype
  - type: key
    id: C4Warhead
  - type: action
    id: TACTION_LIGHT_SMALL
  - type: action
    id: TACTION_LIGHT_LARGE
---

```ini title="map file"
[Actions]
DemoSpark=1,65,0,3,0,0,0,0,D ; trigger DemoSpark flashes at waypoint 3, whose label is D
```

The flash is centered on waypoint `3` (`D`).
