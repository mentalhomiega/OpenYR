---
type: action
id: TACTION_1_SPECIAL
title: Add 1-time special weapon...
summary: Gives the trigger's house one charged shot of a superweapon, and its cameo where that house is the player's.
valid_values:
  - The position of a superweapon in `[SuperWeaponTypes]`, counting the first section listed as `0`. A position past the end of that list can crash the game.
caveats:
  - The weapon goes to the house that owns the trigger.
  - The weapon is ready at once, and no voice announces it. [One shot at a time](/systems/superweapons/#one-shot-at-a-time) covers how a one-time weapon behaves.
  - A house that already holds the weapon is left as it was. The action cannot turn a weapon from a structure into a one-time weapon, and it cannot add a second shot to a one-time weapon the house has not fired yet.
related:

    id: superweapons

    id: TACTION_FULL_SPECIAL

    id: sidebar
