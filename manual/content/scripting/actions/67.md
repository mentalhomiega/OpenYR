  - The action does more than announce. It decides the mission, and no further action is needed to end it.

## When it takes effect

The action does nothing when the player at this machine is already flagged for any of these results:

- a win;
- a loss;
- self-destruction, which follows a surrender in a multiplayer game.

Otherwise the action flags the player to win and starts no [`SavourDelay`](/keys/savourdelay/) pause. The win takes effect at once, normally in the same frame, unless one of these holds it back:

- In a campaign or standalone mission, the player's house still has an [allow-win](/mapping/actions/taction-allowwin/) hold. The win waits until the hold is lifted.
- A result countdown is already running for the player's house, such as the one that removing an [allow-win tag](/mapping/actions/taction-allowwin/#what-brings-the-count-back-down) restarts. The win waits for it to finish.

[The win sequence](/systems/campaign-progression/#the-win-sequence) describes what runs once the win takes effect.
