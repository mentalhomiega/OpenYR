type: action
id: TACTION_METEOR_SHOWER
title: Meteor Shower At...
summary: Scatters a burst of falling meteors around a waypoint in one frame and plays the meteor storm warning.
valid_values:
  - One of the four intensity settings, which sets how many meteors fall and how far they scatter.
  - A scenario waypoint, read from the waypoint label at the end of the line.
caveats:
  - Keep the intensity between `0` and `3`. Any other value reads the meteor count from outside the engine's table of four counts, so the result is unpredictable. A count of zero or below drops nothing, and a large count creates that many meteors in one frame over a correspondingly wider area.
  - Keep both `METLARGE` and `METSMALL` in the rules `[Animations]` list. Each meteor picks one of the two by name, and a meteor that picks a missing one is created from an invalid animation type, which can crash the game.
  - Every meteor lands at the ground height of its own cell. A bridge over the waypoint does not raise them.
  - The meteors are the falling animations `METLARGE` and `METSMALL`, not the voxel meteor that [Meteor Impact At](/mapping/actions/taction-meteor-impact/) drops.

## The burst

The intensity sets a [base count](/reference/enums/meteor-shower/), and zero to two more meteors are added at random. The final count sets both the number of meteors and how far they scatter. Each meteor lands at a random point up to the count × 70 leptons from the center of the waypoint's cell, in either direction along each axis. Seventy leptons is a little over a quarter of a cell, so an Armageddon reaches up to about four and a half cells out and a drizzle stays within one cell.

All the meteors are created on the frame the trigger springs, and each one is `METLARGE` or `METSMALL` at even odds. The settings of the chosen animation decide what the meteor does when it comes down. An animation that is [a meteor](/keys/ismeteor/#scope-animtype) flies in toward the point it was placed at, and its [`Damage`](/keys/damage/#scope-animtype) and [`Warhead`](/keys/warhead/#scope-animtype) set the strike. Changing those two animations changes every shower.
