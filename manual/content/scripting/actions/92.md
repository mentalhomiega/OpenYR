---
type: action
id: TACTION_ACTIVATE_FIRESTORM
title: Activate Firestorm Defense
summary: Raises the trigger house's firestorm wall by firing its firestorm superweapon.
caveats:
  - The action fires the house's copy of the first `Type=Firestorm` superweapon in the rules. If the rules declare none, the action does nothing.
  - The action does nothing when the wall is already up.
  - With [`UseChargeDrain=yes`](/keys/usechargedrain/) on that weapon, a house a human is playing raises its wall only when the weapon is ready. While the weapon is still charging, the action does nothing and spends no charge. Any other house raises its wall at once, even with no charge and no generator.
  - Without `UseChargeDrain=yes`, the action raises the wall only when the house has the weapon and it is ready, and the charge is spent.
related:

    id: superweapons
  - type: action
    id: TACTION_DEACTIVATE_FIRESTORM
  - type: system
    id: laser-fences
---
