---
type: action
id: TACTION_ACTIVATE_FIRESTORM
title: Activate Firestorm Defense
summary: Raises a computer house's firestorm wall at once, with no charge needed and none spent.
caveats:
  - Yuri's Revenge ignores action 92, and so do we unless the rules are Ares rules (they declare armor types in `[ArmorTypes]`). In Ares rules this is the action Ares documents, and it works for the houses a computer controls. A house a player controls is left alone unless [`FirestormTriggerHumans`](/keys/firestormtriggerhumans/) is `yes`.
  - The house needs no firestorm superweapon and no charge, and the superweapon's charge does not change. The wall bars movement through the walls the house owns, as it does when the weapon raises it.
  - The wall drops when the house runs short of power, and a house that is already short of power is left alone. A wall raised by the superweapon is not tied to power by this action.
  - The action does nothing when the wall is already up.
related:
  - type: system
    id: superweapons
  - type: action
    id: TACTION_DEACTIVATE_FIRESTORM
  - type: system
    id: laser-fences
---

```ini title="map file"
[Actions]
WallUp=1,92,0,0,0,0,0,0,A ; the trigger's house raises its firestorm wall
```
