---
type: action
id: TACTION_SET_AMBIENT_STEP
title: Set ambient step...
summary: Replaces the ambient light fade step in the loaded rules, reading the number as the raw bits of a floating-point value.
valid_values:
  - A whole number from `1008981771` to `1148846080`, read as the 32-bit pattern of a single-precision floating-point step from 0.01 to 1000. Decimal text such as `.1` does not give the step it names.
caveats:
  - A decimal such as `.1` is read as `0` and sets a step of zero. Any whole number from `1` to `1008981770` sets a step too small to move the light. Either way the ambient light stops changing, and a fade to a new level never arrives.
  - A negative number breaks the fade. A step between 0 and -0.01 stops the light as a step of zero does. Any other negative number can move the light away from its target at every change, with no limit.
  - A step much larger than 1000 can move the light far out of range.
  - The action changes the rules value [`AmbientChangeStep`](/keys/ambientchangestep/), so it applies to every fade from then on, including an [ion storm](/systems/ion-storms/)'s, and a saved game keeps it. The key page says how long the change lasts.

## Writing a usable step

To set a step, write the whole number whose bit pattern is the single-precision float of that step. Each step moves the ambient level by whole hundredths, rounded down.

| Number to write | Step | Moves the level by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `1028443341` | 0.05 | 5 hundredths |
| `1036831949` | 0.1 | 10 hundredths |
| `1048576000` | 0.25 | 25 hundredths |
| `1056964608` | 0.5 | 50 hundredths |

```ini title="map file"
[Actions]
SlowFade=1,71,0,1036831949,0,0,0,0, ; trigger SlowFade sets a step of 0.1
```

The single-precision float nearest 0.01 is slightly below it, so its bit pattern, `1008981770`, moves the level by nothing. The smallest number that moves the level is `1008981771`.
