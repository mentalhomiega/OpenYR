---
type: action
id: TACTION_MAKE_ENEMY_ONE_WAY
title: Make Enemy (One-Way)...
summary: Turns the trigger's house against the given house, ending any alliance it holds with it.
caveats:
  - If the trigger's house is allied with the given house, the alliance ends on both sides. No message or announcement follows.
  - If only the given house is allied with the trigger's house, that alliance stays.
  - In a skirmish or multiplayer game the action does nothing when either house's country sets `MultiplayPassive=yes`.
  - The action does nothing when the given house is the trigger's house, a country nobody plays, or a spawn house nobody holds.

The trigger's house also gains one point of anger toward the given house, whether or not the two were allied.

When the two houses were not allied, that point can make the given house the [declared enemy](/systems/base-attacked/#anger-and-the-declared-enemy) of the trigger's house. A campaign house has no declared enemy until its anger rises, so this action can give it one.

When the two houses were allied, the trigger's house picks its declared enemy before the alliance ends, so the given house is passed over. The given house can become the declared enemy only the next time the trigger's house picks again, such as when another house next damages one of its objects.

```ini title="map file"
[Actions]
Betrayal=1,118,0,1,0,0,0,0, ; the trigger's house turns against the house at index 1
```
