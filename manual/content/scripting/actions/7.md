## How the group arrives

The action creates every member of the TaskForce outright. No factory builds them and nothing is paid. The members form one team and follow its Script. [`VeteranLevel`](/keys/veteranlevel/) sets their rank, and [`Full`](/keys/full/) fills their Tiberium storage.

If the group includes a transport, every other member rides inside it. When the TaskForce includes an aircraft with passenger room, only such aircraft are transports. Otherwise vehicles with passenger room are. With several transports, all passengers ride in the one listed last in the TaskForce, whatever its passenger limit.

The action tries these deliveries in order and uses the first that applies:

1. **Paradrop.** A [`Droppod=yes`](/keys/droppod-teamtype/) team flies in aboard a paradrop plane and parachutes over the TeamType's [`Waypoint`](/keys/waypoint/), with every member of the team aboard.
2. **Out of a host.** A team of infantry only walks out of a structure, or rides out of a vehicle with passenger room, standing on or next to the waypoint. A vehicle is chosen over a structure, and the host can belong to any house. A vehicle takes the whole group aboard, whatever its passenger limit, and then unloads it. Infantry that cannot leave a structure are destroyed.
3. **Tunneling.** When every member not riding inside a transport travels by tunnel, the group arrives underground and surfaces at or near the waypoint.
4. **Across the map edge.** Any other group enters from the map edge nearest the waypoint, or from its house's [`Edge`](/keys/edge/) when the TeamType has no waypoint.

[`StartingDropships`](/keys/startingdropships/) describes what a dropship carries and what goes wrong when the receiving house is not the player's.

:::caution[A TaskForce that creates nothing delivers nothing]
A TaskForce entry with a count of zero, such as `0=0,E1`, is accepted but creates no object. If every entry has a count of zero, the action delivers nothing. [Reinforcement (team) at waypoint](/mapping/actions/taction-reinforcements-special/) behaves the same way.
:::
