---
type: action
id: TACTION_ALL_ASSIGN_MISSION
title: All Assign Mission...
summary: Gives every infantry, vehicle and aircraft of the trigger's house the given mission.
valid_values:
  - A mission number from the MissionType table.
caveats:
  - Structures are skipped. So is any infantry, vehicle or aircraft that has no strength left, is carried inside a transport, or is otherwise in limbo.
  - A number outside the table leaves the action doing nothing.
related:
  - type: enum
    id: MissionType
  - type: action
    id: TACTION_ALL_HUNT
---

```ini title="map file"
[Actions]
Attack=1,116,0,14,0,0,0,0, ; mission 14 (Hunt) for every infantry, vehicle and aircraft of the trigger's house
```
