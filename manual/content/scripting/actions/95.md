---
type: action
id: TACTION_MULTI_MISSILE
title: Nuke strike...
summary: Drops a nuclear missile straight down onto the waypoint.
caveats:
  - No superweapon, silo, charge or owning house is involved, and nothing is announced. The missile appears 20000 leptons above the waypoint and falls straight down at 100 leptons per frame, so it lands about 200 frames later.
  - The projectile, warhead and damage all come from the weapon named `NukePayload`, the weapon the nuclear missile superweapon drops. Its [`Speed`](/keys/speed/#scope-weapontype) is ignored.
  - Without a `NukePayload` weapon that has a `Projectile=`, the action fires nothing and the debug log says so.
  - Where a bridge spans the waypoint's cell, the missile strikes the bridge deck. If the bridge is gone before the missile arrives, it strikes the ground.
  - The blast credits no attacker, so its kills earn no score and no veterancy and make no house angry. Objects it hits do not spring [Attacked by any house](/mapping/events/tevent-attacked/) or [Destroyed by any house](/mapping/events/tevent-destroyed/).
related:
