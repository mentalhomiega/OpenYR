## What is sold

The action checks every structure in the scenario and sells each one that meets all of these, in this order:

1. It is active.
2. It has been placed on the map.
3. It is not in [limbo](/glossary/#limbo).
4. It has a tag.
5. That tag is attached to this trigger.

Each structure sold plays its construction animation in reverse, like a structure the player sells. The sale refunds [`RefundPercent`](/keys/refundpercent/) of the price to a human player's house and the full price to a computer house.

Three kinds of structure are not removed by the sale:

- A structure with upgrades installed loses only its most recent upgrade, which is refunded. The structure stays, and each later firing removes one more upgrade before the structure itself is sold.
- A service depot with an object parked on it [sells the object instead](/systems/repair/#selling-at-the-pad), and the depot stays.
- A structure that names an [`UndeploysInto=`](/keys/undeploysinto/) vehicle and meets that page's conditions, such as a deployed tick tank or a mobile war factory, turns back into the vehicle. Nothing is refunded unless the vehicle cannot be placed.

## What does not stop a sale

Two settings that keep other sales from happening do not apply here:

- [`Unsellable=yes`](/keys/unsellable/) is tested only when the player orders a sale.
- The sellable field in a structure's map record is read only by [the computer's decision to sell a building it cannot afford to repair](/systems/repair/#when-the-computer-repairs).

One refusal does apply: [a type with no construction artwork cannot be sold](/keys/buildup/#a-type-with-no-construction-artwork-cannot-be-sold) by any route, including this one. For such a structure the action does nothing.

A [firestorm wall](/systems/laser-fences/#selling-a-section) section is the exception. It has no construction artwork either, but the sale takes it off the map at once and refunds nothing. The player can sell a section only while its house's wall is down; this action sells it even while the wall is raised.
