---
type: action
id: TACTION_PREFERRED_TARGET
title: Preferred target...
summary: Stores a target category on the trigger's house, which a computer house's nuke, lightning storm and paradrops aim by.
valid_values:
  - Any quarry category.
caveats:
  - The category is stored on the house that owns the trigger, and a save game keeps it until another Preferred target action changes it.
  - Only a computer house's nuke, lightning storm and paradrops read it, and only while no preferred target cell aims them. Anything, the starting value, leaves them to their usual aim. See [the preferred target](/systems/superweapons/#the-preferred-target).
  - A human house's weapons ignore it. An attack team takes its category from the Attack line of its script, not from the house.
related:

    id: QuarryType
  - type: mission
    id: TMISSION_ATTACK
  - type: system
    id: target-selection
---
