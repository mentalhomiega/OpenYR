---
type: action
id: TACTION_PREFERRED_TARGET
title: Preferred target...
summary: Stores a target category on the trigger's house that nothing acts on.
valid_values:
  - Any quarry category.
caveats:
  - The action has no effect on play. The category is stored on the house that owns the trigger, and nothing in the game reads it back.
  - Superweapon aiming ignores it. An attack team takes its category from the Attack line of its script.
related:

    id: QuarryType

    id: TMISSION_ATTACK
  - type: system
