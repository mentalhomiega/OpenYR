---
type: action
id: TACTION_DEACTIVATE_FIRESTORM
title: Deactivate Firestorm Defense
summary: Lowers a computer house's firestorm wall at once.
caveats:
  - Yuri's Revenge ignores action 93. This is the action Ares documents, meant to end a wall that [Activate Firestorm Defense](/mapping/actions/taction-activate-firestorm/) raised. It lowers a computer house's wall whichever way it was raised, and does not change the superweapon's charge.
  - A house a player controls is left alone unless [`FirestormTriggerHumans`](/keys/firestormtriggerhumans/) is `yes`.
  - The action does nothing when the wall is already down.
related:
