---
type: action
id: TACTION_REVEAL_ZONE
title: Reveal zone of waypoint...
summary: Uncovers the ground within two cells of every playable-area cell that shares the waypoint's crusher movement zone, for every human player.
valid_values:
  - A scenario waypoint, read from the number field of the action line. The waypoint label at the end of the line is not used.
caveats:
  - A line whose number field is `0` works from waypoint `0`, whatever label ends the line. If the scenario places no waypoint `0`, the action uses the zone of the cell at `0,0`, which lies outside the playfield.
  - The action checks only cells inside the playable area. Ground of the same zone in the map border is uncovered only where it lies within two cells of a matching playable-area cell.
  - The zone is the one a crusher paths through, which can differ from the zone other kinds of object use. Distance from the waypoint does not matter.
  - With RevealByHeight=yes, the height test treats each matching cell as standing at half its real height. Ground of the zone seven or more levels up can therefore stay covered.
  - The action does nothing for a player whose map is already marked as fully revealed by Reveal all map, the reveal team mission or the reveal crate. Reshrouding the map clears the mark.
related:

    id: LocalSize

    id: TACTION_REVEAL_ALL

---

```ini title="map file"
[Actions]
DemoZone=1,18,0,4,0,0,0,0,E ; trigger DemoZone uncovers the landmass holding waypoint 4, whose label is E
```

The number `4` is the waypoint whose zone the action reads, and `E` is its label.

The reveal lifts both the shroud and the fog, and the [height test](/keys/revealbyheight/) applies around each matching cell. [Map visibility](/systems/map-visibility/#reveals-granted-outright) compares this reveal with the others.
