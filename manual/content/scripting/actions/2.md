---
type: action
id: TACTION_LOSE
title: Loser is...
summary: Loses the mission for the player at this machine when the house named is theirs, and wins it otherwise.
valid_values:
  - A house by number, either a country or a [spawn house](/formats/scenario-objects/#spawn-houses). A country names the player when the player plays that country. A spawn house names the player when the player holds that start position.
caveats:
  - Any house that does not name the player at this machine flags that player to win. That includes a number that names no house at all.
  - The house named is not touched. It is neither defeated nor destroyed, keeps everything it owns, and goes on playing. Naming an opposing house wins the mission for the player; it does not wipe that house out.
  - The loss or win takes effect after the [`SavourDelay`](/keys/savourdelay/) pause. The caption and the spoken line come at once, except for an observer in a skirmish or multiplayer game. In a campaign or single mission, the player's controls are also locked at once.
  - The win or loss this action gives can be ignored when the player already has a result pending. [`SavourDelay`](/keys/savourdelay/) lists which pending results ignore it.
related:

    id: TACTION_WIN
  - type: mission
    id: TMISSION_LOSE
  - type: system
    id: campaign-progression
  - type: key
    id: SavourDelay
---

The action decides the mission only for the player at this machine. It compares the house named only with that player, so it settles nothing between other houses. In a multiplayer game, each machine makes the comparison for its own player, and one firing can tell one player they lost and every other player they won.

In a campaign or single mission, a loss plays the losing movie and offers a replay, as [losing and restarting](/systems/campaign-progression/#losing-and-restarting) describes.
