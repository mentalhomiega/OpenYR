---
type: action
id: TACTION_FORCE_END
title: Force end
summary: Wins the mission for the player at this machine after the SavourDelay pause, unless the player's result is already decided.
caveats:
  - The player at this machine wins, whichever house owns the trigger, with the same caption, EVA line and control lock as [Announce Win](/mapping/actions/taction-announce-win/).
  - In a multiplayer game, every player wins, because the action runs on every machine and flags the player at that machine.
  - Unlike Announce Win, this action starts the [`SavourDelay`](/keys/savourdelay/) pause, so the mission runs on for that long before the win takes effect. In a campaign or standalone mission, the win also waits for any [allow-win](/mapping/actions/taction-allowwin/) hold to be lifted.
  - When the player is already flagged to win, to lose, or to self-destruct after a surrender in a multiplayer game, the action does nothing. The pending result and its timing stand.

    id: TACTION_ANNOUNCE_WIN

    id: TACTION_ANNOUNCE_LOSE

    id: SavourDelay
