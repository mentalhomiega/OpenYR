---
type: action
id: TACTION_PLAY_SPEECH
title: Play speech...
summary: Adds one speech line to the queue of lines waiting to be spoken.
caveats:
  - The line joins the queue of waiting lines. [EVA speech](/systems/eva-speech/#order) gives the order they play in.
  - Two Play speech actions in the same trigger both play, one after the other, as long as they name different lines and a full queue does not drop the first. [EVA speech](/systems/eva-speech/#order) says which line a full queue drops.
  - A line that is already speaking or already waiting is not queued again.
  - Nothing is spoken while the speech volume is zero.
  - While [Disable Speech](/mapping/actions/taction-disable-speech/) is in force, this action still plays any line that is not an EVA line. [Turning EVA off](/systems/eva-speech/#turning-eva-off) says which lines count as EVA.

    id: TACTION_ENABLE_SPEECH
