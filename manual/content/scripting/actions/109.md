---
type: action
id: TACTION_CREATE_BUILDING_AT
title: Create Building At...
summary: Places a building of the given type for the given house at the action's waypoint.
valid_values:
  - A house by number, either a country or a spawn house. A BuildingType by its index in the loaded `[BuildingTypes]` list. A force flag of `0` or `1`. A waypoint the map has placed, written as its letter label.
caveats:
  - A house nobody plays, a spawn house nobody holds, an index outside the list, or a waypoint the map has not placed leaves the action doing nothing.
  - A line that leaves out the waypoint field places the building at waypoint `A`.
  - The house needs no prerequisites or tech level for the type.
related:
  - type: format
    id: scenario-objects
---

The force flag decides how the building is placed:

- With the flag at `0`, the building is refused unless every cell of its foundation is clear to build on. It does not need to be next to the house's base. A structure that is placed plays its build-up animation. Walls and upgrades appear at once.
- With the flag at `1` or above, the foundation is not checked, so the building can be placed on blocked or unsuitable ground. It appears complete, with no build-up animation. A wall is still refused on some sloped cells.

Either way, an upgrade is placed only onto a structure of the same house that stands on the waypoint and is the type the upgrade is for. The structure must also have room for it:

- An upgrade with a [`PowersUpToLevel`](/keys/powersuptolevel/) level fits only a structure with no upgrades.
- Any other upgrade fits a structure with no upgrades, or one holding fewer than its [`Upgrades`](/keys/upgrades/) count.

```ini title="map file"
[Waypoints]
1=45060 ; waypoint 1 has the letter label B

[Actions]
Outpost=1,109,0,0,12,1,0,0,B ; the house at index 0 receives BuildingType 12 at waypoint B, forced
```

[Spawn houses](/formats/scenario-objects/#spawn-houses) lists the house numbers that name a start position in place of a country. When several houses play the named country, only one of them receives the building.
