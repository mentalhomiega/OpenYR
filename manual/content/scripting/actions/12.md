## The tag is left behind

When a trigger is removed, its tag moves on to the next trigger in its chain. A tag that held only this trigger, the usual shape, is left with no trigger at all. It stays on its object or cell and springs nothing for the rest of the scenario.

An [Allow Win](/mapping/actions/taction-allowwin/) tag emptied this way can never lift its hold on the victory. Destroying the tag afterward leaves the hold in place. [Destroying the tag instead](/mapping/actions/taction-allowwin/#destroying-the-tag-instead) explains why.
