---
type: action
id: TACTION_BEGIN_AI_TRIGGERS
title: AI triggers begin...
summary: Turns on the AI-trigger switch of the house named, so that house starts raising teams from its AI triggers.
valid_values:
  - A live house selected by a [house number](/formats/scenario-objects/#spawn-houses).
caveats:
  - AI triggers are the only source of the teams a house raises on its own. Until this switch is on, the house raises none.
  - The switch starts off for every house, and in a campaign game only this action turns it on. [When the pass runs](/systems/ai-team-production/#when-the-pass-runs) lists what changes it in other games.
  - With the switch on, the house draws from its AI triggers only when its [`RatioAITriggerTeam`](/keys/ratioaitriggerteam/) roll passes.
  - The action also turns the switch on for a human house. That house then raises teams from its AI triggers but orders no production to fill them.
  - A house nobody plays, or a spawn house nobody holds, leaves the action doing nothing. The trigger's other actions still run.
related:

    id: TACTION_STOP_AI_TRIGGERS

    id: TACTION_SET_AI_TRIGGER_TEAM_RATIO

    id: RatioAITriggerTeam
