---
type: action
id: TACTION_VEIN_GROWTH
title: Vein growth...
summary: Turns the scenario's vein growth switch on or off, which decides whether veinholes grow their vein fields.
caveats:
  - Only the lowest byte of the number is tested. `256` or any other multiple of `256` turns growth off, and every other non-zero number turns it on.
  - The action writes the same switch as the map's [`VeinGrowthEnabled`](/keys/veingrowthenabled/) key. The new setting lasts until something writes the switch again, and a saved game keeps it.
  - Turning growth off stops every growth step, including the regrowth of harvested cells. The field of a destroyed veinhole still [withers](/systems/veins/#destruction) while the switch is off.
