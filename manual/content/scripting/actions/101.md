---
type: action
id: TACTION_FLASH_TEAM
title: Flash Team...
summary: Flashes the members of every live team built from the named TeamType.
caveats:
  - If no team built from the named TeamType is on the map, the action does nothing.
  - Only the infantry, vehicles and aircraft in a team when the action runs flash. A structure is never a team member. Members that join later do not flash, and members that leave keep flashing until their duration runs out.
  - Use a positive duration. A negative one makes the members flash for the rest of the mission.
related:
  - type: mission
    id: TMISSION_FLASH
  - type: action
    id: TACTION_TALK_BUBBLE
---
