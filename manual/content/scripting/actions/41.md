---
type: action
id: TACTION_PLAY_ANIM
title: Play Anim At
summary: Plays an animation at a waypoint without the damage the animation itself deals while it plays.
valid_values:
  - An animation's position in the registered animation list, counted from `0`, and a waypoint the map places. The waypoint is the name at the end of the line.
caveats:
  - The animation deals none of its [`Damage`](/keys/damage/#scope-animtype) while it plays. Its other effects still happen. Its [`Report`](/keys/report/#scope-animtype) sound plays, a [`TiberiumChainReaction=yes`](/keys/tiberiumchainreaction/) animation sets off the Tiberium on its cell, and a [`Bouncer=yes`](/keys/bouncer/) or [`IsMeteor=yes`](/keys/ismeteor/#scope-animtype) animation deals its damage where it lands.
  - Animations it creates deal their own [`Damage`](/keys/damage/#scope-animtype). These include the fires a [`Scorch=yes`](/keys/scorch/) or [`Flamer=yes`](/keys/flamer/) animation starts and its [`TrailerAnim`](/keys/traileranim/#scope-animtype) trail.
  - The animation is chosen by position, so adding, removing or reordering a line in the rules `[Animations]` list shifts every number already stored in a map.
  - The position is used without a range check. One at or beyond the end of the list reads past it.
related:
  - type: action
    id: TACTION_DO_EXPLOSION
---

The animation plays at the waypoint's ground height, raised by the bridge height where a bridge covers or has covered the cell.

```ini title="map file"
[Waypoints]
12=80080 ; waypoint 12, written as the letter label M in an action

[Actions]
DemoPlayAnim=1,41,0,7,0,0,0,0,M ; trigger DemoPlayAnim plays the animation at position 7 at waypoint M
```
