---
type: action
id: TACTION_CHANGE_ZOOM
title: Change Zoom Level...
summary: Does nothing; no zoom level changes.
valid_values:
  - Any number, including the `1` and `2` the [action list](/mapping/actions/) suggests. No value has an effect.
caveats:
  - The number is read from the map file, but nothing uses it.
  - The radar map's zoom does not change either, although the action list says it does. The player switches the radar pane's display with [Radar Toggle](/commands/toggleradar/), which does nothing in a campaign or standalone mission.
