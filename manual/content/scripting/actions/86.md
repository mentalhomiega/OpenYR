type: action
id: TACTION_TIB_GROWTH
title: Tiberium growth...
summary: Turns Tiberium growth and spread on or off while the scenario runs.
caveats:
  - The action sets the same switch as [`TiberiumGrowthEnabled`](/keys/tiberiumgrowthenabled/) in the map's `[Basic]` section, and that page describes what the switch stops. The new setting lasts until the action runs again with another value.
  - Use `1` to turn growth on and `0` to turn it off. The action reads only the lowest byte of the number, so `256` and its multiples also turn growth off.
