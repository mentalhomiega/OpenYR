---
type: action
id: TACTION_CHEM_MISSILE
title: Chem-missile strike...
summary: Flies a chemical missile in from the nearest map edge and detonates it on the waypoint.
caveats:
  - No superweapon, silo, charge or owning house is involved, and nothing is announced. The missile appears at the map edge nearest the waypoint, as in [Nuke strike](/mapping/actions/taction-multi-missile/).
  - The projectile, warhead, damage and [`ProjectileRange`](/keys/projectilerange/) all come from the weapon named `ChemLauncher`.
  - Keep a weapon named `ChemLauncher` in the rules; without one, the action can crash the game.
  - The missile's top speed is fixed at 20 leptons per frame, about `Speed=8` on a weapon's 0 to 100 scale. The [`Speed`](/keys/speed/#scope-weapontype) of `ChemLauncher` is ignored.
  - Where a bridge spans the waypoint's cell, the missile strikes the bridge deck. If the bridge is gone before the missile arrives, it strikes the ground.
  - The blast credits no attacker, so its kills earn no score and no veterancy and make no house angry. Objects it hits do not spring [Attacked by any house](/mapping/events/tevent-attacked/) or [Destroyed by any house](/mapping/events/tevent-destroyed/).
