---
type: action
id: TACTION_GIVE_CREDITS
title: Give Credits...
summary: Adds the given number of credits to a house, or takes that many away when the number is negative.
valid_values:
  - A house by number, either a country or a spawn house, and a whole number of credits.
caveats:
  - A house nobody plays, or a spawn house nobody holds, leaves the action doing nothing.
  - When several houses play the named country, only one of them is affected. Name a spawn house to choose a particular player.
  - A negative amount is charged like a purchase. The house's credits are spent first, down to zero. The Tiberium stored in its silos and refineries then pays the rest at its credit value. Any shortfall after that is dropped.
  - Keep the amount between `-2147483647` and `2147483647`. A gain that would take the house's credits above `2147483647` is refused.
related:
  - type: format
    id: scenario-objects
  - type: event
    id: TEVENT_CREDITS
---

The house is written as a number. [Spawn houses](/formats/scenario-objects/#spawn-houses) lists the numbers that name a start position in place of a country.

The amount goes in the first of the four fields after the house:

```ini title="map file"
[Actions]
Bonus=1,106,0,0,5000,0,0,0, ; the house at index 0 gains 5000 credits
Fine=1,106,0,0,-2000,0,0,0, ; and loses 2000
```
