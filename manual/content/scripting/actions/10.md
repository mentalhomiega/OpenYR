---
type: action
id: TACTION_PLAY_MOVIE
title: Play Movie...
summary: Plays a movie over the whole screen and resumes the mission when it ends.
caveats:
  - Outside a campaign, the movie plays only when the launch file sets `PlayMoviesInMultiplayer=yes`. Otherwise a skirmish or multiplayer game skips it and goes on with the trigger's remaining actions.
  - A movie whose file is missing or cannot be opened is skipped the same way. So is a movie narrower than 320 pixels and shorter than 200.
  - Escape ends the movie early and the mission resumes. No other key does. In a game against other machines, the movie stops only after every player and observer has pressed Escape.
  - The screen turns black before and after the movie, and the whole display is redrawn when the mission resumes.
related:
  - type: format
    id: vqa
  - type: action
    id: TACTION_PLAY_INGAME_MOVIE
---

[Multiplayer movies](/systems/multiplayer-movies/) covers how the launch file turns movies on outside a campaign, and how the players of a game against other machines [vote to skip](/systems/multiplayer-movies/#skipping-a-movie-together) one.

## What happens to the music

The music keeps playing. A track that is playing when the movie starts goes on under the movie's soundtrack. The action neither queues a track nor stops one.
