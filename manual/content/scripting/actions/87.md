---
type: action
id: TACTION_ICE_GROWTH
title: Ice growth...
summary: Turns ice growth and the refreezing of cracked ice on or off while the scenario runs.
caveats:
  - The action sets the same switch as [`IceGrowthEnabled`](/keys/icegrowthenabled/) in the map's `[Basic]` section, and that page describes what the switch stops. The new setting lasts until the action runs again with another value.
  - The action has a visible effect only in a theater whose [`IsIceGrowthEnabled`](/keys/isicegrowthenabled/) is `yes`, which by default is only the snow theater.
  - Use `1` to turn the switch on and `0` to turn it off. The action reads only the lowest byte of the number, so `256` and its multiples also turn the switch off.
