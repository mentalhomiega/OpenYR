---
type: action
id: TACTION_STOP_TIMER
title: Timer Stop
summary: Pauses the mission countdown at its current count, and EVA announces it.
caveats:
  - The action does nothing while the countdown is not running.
  - The count is kept. [Timer Start](/mapping/actions/taction-start-timer/) resumes from it. [Timer Extend](/mapping/actions/taction-add-timer/) and [Timer Shorten](/mapping/actions/taction-sub-timer/) also start the countdown running again.
  - The clock disappears while the countdown is stopped, so the player cannot see a paused count.
