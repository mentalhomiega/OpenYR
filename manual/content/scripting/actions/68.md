type: action
id: TACTION_ANNOUNCE_LOSE
title: Announce Lose
summary: Loses the mission for the player at this machine without the usual SavourDelay pause.
caveats:
  - The action does more than announce. It decides the mission, and no further action is needed to end it.
  - The player at this machine loses, whichever house owns the trigger.
  - In a multiplayer game, every player loses, because the action runs on every machine and flags the player at that machine.
  - A pending win is cancelled and the loss replaces it. This includes a win that [Announce Win](/mapping/actions/taction-announce-win/) flagged and a win that an [allow-win](/mapping/actions/taction-allowwin/) hold is delaying.
  - The action does nothing when the player is already flagged to lose, or to self-destruct after a surrender in a multiplayer game.
  - The action starts no [`SavourDelay`](/keys/savourdelay/) pause, so the loss takes effect at once, normally in the same frame. A result countdown already running for the player's house, such as the one that removing an [allow-win tag](/mapping/actions/taction-allowwin/#what-brings-the-count-back-down) restarts, finishes first.
  - In a campaign or standalone mission, the player's controls are locked and the mission-failed caption and EVA line play. In skirmish and multiplayer games, the defeat caption and EVA line play instead.
