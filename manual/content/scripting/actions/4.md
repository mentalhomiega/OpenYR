---
type: action
id: TACTION_CREATE_TEAM
title: Create Team...
summary: Creates one team of the TeamType named, starting with no members.
valid_values:
  - A TeamType the scenario declares. A line naming no team runs and does nothing.
caveats:
  - The team belongs to the house the TeamType names, not to the trigger's house as the [action list](/mapping/actions/) says.
  - The TeamType's [`Max`](/keys/max/) limit does not apply, so each firing creates another team, even for a type with `Max=0`.
  - The action creates no objects. The team recruits objects its house already owns, and a computer house also orders production to fill it.
  - A TeamType that names a [`Tag`](/keys/tag/) gives each team created from it a separate tag, so two teams of one type have independent tags.
related:

    id: TACTION_DESTROY_TEAM

  - type: key
    id: House
  - type: key
    id: Max

    id: TaskForce

    id: ai-team-production
  - type: system
    id: ai-team-execution
---

To have one side's trigger create another side's team, name that side's country in the TeamType's [`House=`](/keys/house/). If no house in the match plays that country, or the TeamType names a [spawn house](/formats/scenario-objects/#spawn-houses) nobody holds, the action creates no team.

[Recruitment](/systems/ai-team-production/#recruitment) covers how the empty team fills, and [Production demand](/systems/ai-team-production/#production-demand) covers what a computer house builds for it. [Team scripts](/systems/ai-team-execution/) cover what the team does once it has enough members to start.
