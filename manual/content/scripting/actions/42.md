  - A position in the rules weapon list, counted from `0`, and a scenario waypoint.
caveats:
  - The weapon is chosen by position, not by name. Adding, removing or reordering a line in the rules `[Weapons]` list shifts every number already stored in a map. A weapon that list leaves out is numbered where it is first named instead.

  - The blast has no firer. The damage it deals raises no house's anger, no house is credited with its kills, and nothing gains veterancy from them.

## What is detonated

Only the weapon's [`Damage`](/keys/damage/#scope-weapontype) and [`Warhead`](/keys/warhead/#scope-weapontype) are used. Its range, rate of fire, projectile, sounds and firing animation have no effect. The blast happens at the waypoint's cell at ground height, raised by the bridge height where a bridge covers or has covered that cell.

The blast has these effects, in this order:

1. The warhead's explosion animation for that amount of damage on that terrain plays, if the warhead has one.
2. The lighting flash for that damage and warhead is applied.
3. The damage is dealt, on the same terms as any other blast from that warhead.

A weapon whose ID is `empulseweapon`, in any letter case, also creates [an EM pulse](/systems/emp-pulse/#scripted-explosion) after the blast.

A weapon written only for this action still needs a line in the [`[Weapons]` list](/formats/rules-registries/). A weapon section that nothing names is never registered, so no position selects it.

## Trigger events the blast does not spring

An object the blast damages does not spring [Half health (combat only)](/mapping/events/tevent-enter-yellow/), [Quarter health (combat only)](/mapping/events/tevent-enter-red/) or [First damaged (combat only)](/mapping/events/tevent-first-damaged/) on its tag, even when the blast destroys it. [Half health (any source)](/mapping/events/tevent-enter-yellow-any/), [Quarter health (any source)](/mapping/events/tevent-enter-red-any/) and [First damaged (any source)](/mapping/events/tevent-first-damaged-any/) still spring.

An object that survives the blast also does not spring [Attacked by any house](/mapping/events/tevent-attacked/) or [Attacked by (house)...](/mapping/events/tevent-attacked-by/).

An object the blast destroys does not spring [Destroyed by any house](/mapping/events/tevent-destroyed/), [Attacked by any house](/mapping/events/tevent-attacked/) or [Discovered by player](/mapping/events/tevent-discovered/) on its tag. [Destroyed by anything](/mapping/events/tevent-destroyed-any/) and [its no-infiltrate form](/mapping/events/tevent-destroyed-any-x/) still spring.
