---
type: action
id: TACTION_TURN_OFF_ATTACHED
title: Turn off building
summary: Takes every structure whose tag is attached to this trigger out of service.
caveats:
  - Every structure in the scenario whose tag is attached to this trigger is switched off, not only the structure that sprang it. A structure in limbo, or one not yet placed on the map, is skipped.
  - Only a structure that is switched on and whose type has a negative `Power=` or is `Powered=yes` is affected. Other tagged structures keep running.
  - The action ignores `TogglePower`, so it can switch off a structure the player cannot switch back on.
  - Switching off has the same effects as the player's power toggle, listed under [switching a structure off](/systems/power/#switching-a-structure-off).
  - A house under player control hears the structure-offline announcement. Several structures switched off by one firing produce one announcement.
