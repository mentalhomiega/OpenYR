---
type: action
id: TACTION_LIGHT_SMALL
title: Light flash (small) at...
summary: Briefly lights the ground around a waypoint with the smallest flash, and harms nothing there.
valid_values:
  - A scenario waypoint, written as its number in the fourth field of the line. Waypoint `A` is `0`. The action ignores the waypoint label at the end of the line.
caveats:
  - No flash appears unless the warhead that `C4Warhead` names sets `Bright=yes`.
  - Nothing is damaged, moved or destroyed. The light is the whole effect.
  - This is the smallest flash the engine draws. The medium flash is only about a fifth wider, and the large flash is about three times as wide.
  - The flash brightens and fades within about ten game frames. It is not drawn while fog of war covers the waypoint.
related:
  - type: key
    id: Bright
    scope: warheadtype
  - type: key
    id: C4Warhead
  - type: action
    id: TACTION_LIGHT_MEDIUM
  - type: action
    id: TACTION_LIGHT_LARGE
---

```ini title="map file"
[Actions]
DemoSpark=1,64,0,7,0,0,0,0,H ; trigger DemoSpark flashes at waypoint 7, whose label is H
```

The flash is centered on waypoint `7` (`H`).
