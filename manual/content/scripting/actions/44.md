---
type: action
id: TACTION_ION_STORM_START
title: Ion Storm start...
summary: Schedules an ion storm that runs for the given number of seconds.
valid_values:
  - A number of seconds. The storm breaks after the warning countdown and ends that many seconds later.
caveats:
  - The number is in seconds, although the [action list](/mapping/actions/) says game frames. The [team mission that starts a storm](/mapping/missions/tmission-ion-storm-start/) takes its number in frames.
  - The action does nothing while a storm is already running. [The warning](/systems/ion-storms/#the-warning) describes what a second start during the countdown does.
related:
  - type: system
    id: ion-storms
  - type: mission
    id: TMISSION_ION_STORM_START
---

The storm breaks after a [warning countdown](/systems/ion-storms/#the-warning) of [`IonStormWarning`](/keys/ionstormwarning/) seconds, or on the frame the action runs when that is `0`. [The storm breaks](/systems/ion-storms/#the-storm-breaks) lists its effects.

```ini title="map file"
[Actions]
DemoIonStorm=1,44,0,20,0,0,0,0, ; trigger DemoIonStorm runs a 20-second storm
```
