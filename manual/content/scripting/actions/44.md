---
type: action
id: TACTION_ION_STORM_START
title: Ion Storm start...
summary: Calls a lightning storm over a random cell that rages for the given number of game frames.
valid_values:
  - A number of game frames. The storm breaks after the warning countdown and rages that many frames.
caveats:
  - The storm is the [lightning storm](/systems/superweapons/#lightning-storm) of the Lightning Storm super weapon, not the [ion storm](/systems/ion-storms/) of earlier games. It breaks [`LightningDeferment`](/keys/lightningdeferment/) frames after the action runs, and its center is a random cell on the map.
  - No house owns the storm, so every house loses its radar while it rages. A storm called by [Lightning Storm strike...](/mapping/actions/taction-lightning-storm-strike/) credits its kills to the house of the trigger instead.
  - The number is in game frames, not seconds. A value of `-1` gives a storm that lasts until [Ion Storm stop...](/mapping/actions/taction-ion-storm-stop/) or the [Ion storn end](/mapping/missions/tmission-ion-storm-end/) team line runs.
  - The action fails while a storm is raging. A storm that is still counting down does not stop another call, and the later call replaces the earlier one's duration and center while the earlier countdown goes on.
related:
  - type: action
    id: TACTION_LIGHTNING_STORM_STRIKE
  - type: mission
    id: TMISSION_ION_STORM_START
  - type: system
    id: superweapons
---

```ini title="map file"
[Actions]
DemoStorm=1,44,0,900,0,0,0,0, ; trigger DemoStorm calls a storm that rages for 900 frames
```
