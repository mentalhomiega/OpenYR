---
type: action
id: TACTION_PLAY_SOUND_RANDOM
title: Play Sound Effect (Random)...
summary: Plays a sound effect at a randomly chosen waypoint among those the scenario places.
caveats:
  - Every waypoint the scenario places can be chosen, including the home, reinforcement and special waypoints. Waypoints the scenario does not place are skipped.
  - The sound is a [placed sound](/systems/sound-effects/#placed-sounds), so it fades and pans with the chosen waypoint's position relative to the view.
  - The waypoint is drawn from the game's synchronized random numbers, so every machine in a multiplayer game chooses the same waypoint.
  - Leave at least one of the waypoints `0` through `100` unplaced. The action has room for 100 waypoints, and in a scenario that places all 101 it writes past the end of its list, which can corrupt memory.
related:

    id: TACTION_PLAY_SOUND

---

## With no waypoint placed

Place at least one waypoint before using this action. In a scenario with no placed waypoint, the action still picks a position for the sound. About half the time it is cell `0,0`, and otherwise an arbitrary position taken from unrelated memory. The sound is heard only if that position is within range of the view.
