---
type: action
id: TACTION_ION_STORM_STOP
title: Ion Storm stop...
summary: Ends the lightning storm that is raging.
caveats:
  - The action fails unless a storm is raging. A storm still counting down breaks on schedule, however many times this action runs during the countdown.
  - Clouds already over the map finish their animation, so the lightning and the dark lighting go on for a few seconds after the action runs. The storm counts as over when the last cloud has gone.
  - Ending a storm does not prevent a later one. Another [Ion Storm start...](/mapping/actions/taction-ion-storm-start/) or [Lightning Storm strike...](/mapping/actions/taction-lightning-storm-strike/) action, or the Lightning Storm super weapon, calls a new storm as usual.
related:
  - type: action
    id: TACTION_ION_STORM_START
  - type: mission
    id: TMISSION_ION_STORM_END
  - type: system
    id: superweapons
---

The action ends any lightning storm that is raging, whatever called it.
