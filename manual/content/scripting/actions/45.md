---
type: action
id: TACTION_ION_STORM_STOP
title: Ion Storm stop...
summary: Ends an ion storm that has already broken.
caveats:
  - The action does nothing unless a storm has already broken. A storm still in its warning countdown breaks on schedule, however many times this action runs during the countdown.
  - Ending a storm does not prevent a later one. Another Ion Storm start... action or Ion storm start in... team mission starts a new storm as usual.
related:
  - type: action
    id: TACTION_ION_STORM_START
  - type: mission
    id: TMISSION_ION_STORM_END
  - type: key
    id: IonStormWarning
  - type: system
    id: ion-storms
---

To call off a storm started by [Ion Storm start...](/mapping/actions/taction-ion-storm-start/), run this action after the storm breaks. The storm breaks [`IonStormWarning`](/keys/ionstormwarning/) seconds after it is started. With `IonStormWarning=0`, it breaks as soon as it is started, so there is no countdown for this action to miss.

[The storm ends](/systems/ion-storms/#the-storm-ends) lists what is restored when the storm ends, and what is not.
