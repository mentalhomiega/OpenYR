---
command_id: RepeatLastBuilding
---

Starts another structure of the same type as the player's newest structure, as a click on its cameo would. The newest structure is the one the player placed most recently, unless another of the player's structures has appeared on the map since in some other way, such as a vehicle deploying into it. In a campaign, a structure of the player's that appears out of the player's sight, including one hidden when the mission starts, becomes the newest when the player first sees it. The command acts only while the [sidebar](/systems/sidebar/) still offers that type.

Structures do not queue. While a structure is under construction, on hold, or finished and waiting to be placed, the command is refused with the voice line for a busy factory.

Nothing happens until one of the player's structures has become the newest in the current game. Structures the map starts with do not count, except in a campaign, where one hidden at the start counts when the player first sees it.
