---
command_id: AttackMove
---

Arms an attack-move for the selected units. The next left click on the ground orders each selected unit to travel to that cell and to engage an enemy it reaches on the way. After the enemy is gone, the unit resumes travel to the cell. The arm ends with that click, so the next order is a normal move.

Holding Control and Shift with a left click on the ground gives the same order without arming first. The [Command bar](/commands/) attack-move button arms the order in the same way as the key.

A click on an enemy object still attacks it. Only ground orders are attack-moves; a click on a friendly object gives its usual order.

The default key is A in the Modern control scheme. The Classic scheme leaves the command unbound.
