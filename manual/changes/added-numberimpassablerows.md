`NumberImpassableRows` is now read. A vehicle in radio contact with a structure, and any vehicle moving through a service depot or bunker, is stopped only by the first columns of its foundation. Stock rules set it on the refineries, factories, naval yards, service depots, and the tank bunker, so vehicles can drive into their rear columns.
