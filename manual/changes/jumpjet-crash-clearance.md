---
title: Let a jumpjet stand on a bridge deck within its crash clearance
category: fix
release: 0.2.0
targets:
- type: key
  id: JumpjetCrash
  effect: added
credit: [MentalHomiega]
---

A jumpjet now stands on a bridge deck when it is within `JumpjetCrash` leptons below the deck. The key defaults to 5, as in the original game. Before, a jumpjet had to be at the deck's height to be on it.
