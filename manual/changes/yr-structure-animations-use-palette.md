---
title: Draw structure animations through Palette=
category: feature
release: 0.2.0
targets:
- type: key
  id: Palette
  effect: added
credit: [MentalHomiega]
---

A structure with `Palette=` in its art now draws its cell-drawn animations and its destroy animation through that palette, in its house's colors. The Statue of Liberty sets `Palette=lib` in the stock art, so its destroy animation changes on a temperate map. Airstrike and Force Shield tints do not reach these animations.
