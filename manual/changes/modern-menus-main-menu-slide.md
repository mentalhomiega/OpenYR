With the modern [menu style](/keys/menustyle/), the Main Menu, Single Player and Movies & Credits menus now appear whole and without the slide-open. Before, the slide showed only a narrow slice of the panel for the first moments, because the panel stands at the left of the screen. The classic style still slides its menus open from the middle.
