---
title: Show the attack-move cursor for an armed attack-move
category: fix
release: 0.2.0
targets:
- type: command
  id: AttackMove
  effect: changed
credit:
- MentalHomiega
---

An armed attack-move, the held attack-move key, or Control with Shift over units that can all attack-move now shows the attack-move cursor, a nine-frame ring, over ground and over enemies, in reach or not, shrouded or not. Before, ground and out-of-reach enemies showed the five-frame attack cursor, and an enemy in reach showed the stay-attack cursor. Control with Shift showed no attack cursor before. The click gives the same order as before.
