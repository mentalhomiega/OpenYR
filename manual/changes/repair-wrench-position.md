The repair wrench is drawn at the north corner of the structure's first cell, over the structure and its health bar, and nowhere else. It no longer moves up and to the left while the structure is switched off, and it shows on a structure under fog the way it does in Yuri's Revenge, hiding only under shroud. A structure that is switched off no longer shows the second, power-off icon, which Yuri's Revenge never draws.
