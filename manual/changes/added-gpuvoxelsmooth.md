title: Add GpuVoxelSmooth for drawing GPU voxel objects at their exact facing
category: feature
release: 0.2.0
targets:
- type: key
  id: GpuVoxelSmooth
  effect: added
credit:
- MentalHomiega
---

[`GpuVoxelSmooth=yes`](/keys/gpuvoxelsmooth/) turns the objects that `GpuVoxels` draws on the GPU to their exact facing instead of the nearest of 32. It is off by default.
