---
title: Keep warhead Verses when a map redefines a warhead
category: feature
release: 0.2.0
targets:
- type: key
  id: KeepWarheadVerses
  effect: added
credit: [MentalHomiega]
---

`KeepWarheadVerses=` decides whether a warhead redefined without `Verses=`, usually by a map, keeps its rules Verses. Rules that declare Ares armor types keep them by default; stock rules still reset them to 100%.
