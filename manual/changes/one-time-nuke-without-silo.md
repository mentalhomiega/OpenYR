A nuclear missile that a trigger grants as a one-time weapon now falls on its target from the sky, as in Yuri's Revenge, with the psychic warning marker over the target cell. It drops the `NukePayload` weapon straight down from 20000 leptons up. Before, the game crashed when the missile was fired.
