[`ClassicGameSpeeds`](/keys/classicgamespeeds/) gives a skirmish the frame rates of Yuri's Revenge's single player game, 62.5 to 10.4 frames a second with the Fastest setting unlimited, and runs every campaign mission at 31.25 frames a second with no speed slider. The default `no` uses the multiplayer rates, 60 to 10 frames a second, in both. The **Original game speeds** switch on the game controls dialog sets the key.
