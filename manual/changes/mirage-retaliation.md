---
title: Do not fire back at a disguised Mirage tank
category: fix
release: 0.2.0
targets:
- type: system
  id: disguises
  effect: changed
credit: [MentalHomiega]
---

A unit that a Mirage tank hits no longer fires back while the tank is disguised to that unit's house, which means the house is not allied with the tank's house and no disguise detector of the house covers the tank's cell. Before, the hit unit turned on the tank and revealed where it stood.
