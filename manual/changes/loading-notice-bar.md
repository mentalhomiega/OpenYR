With the modern [menu style](/keys/menustyle/), loading a mission, a skirmish or network game, or a saved game now shows a box at the centre of the screen over the loading picture. The box names the part of the load under way, such as Reading the rules... or Placing objects..., above a bar whose filled length shows how far the load has come. The classic style keeps the original loading screens.
