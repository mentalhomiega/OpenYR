A spy now goes into `Spyable=yes` structures and, as in Yuri's Revenge, shrouds its victim's map from a radar, cuts its power from a power plant, steals money from a refinery, steals technology from a battle lab, restarts a superweapon, and makes its own house's new vehicles or infantry veterans from a war factory or barracks. Spying on a radar no longer shares the victim's sight.
