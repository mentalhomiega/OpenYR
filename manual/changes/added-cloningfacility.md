---
title: Copy vehicles in cloning structures (CloningFacility)
category: feature
release: 0.2.0
targets:
- type: key
  id: CloningFacility
  effect: added
credit: [MentalHomiega]
---

A structure with `CloningFacility=yes` now releases a free copy of each vehicle its owner's factories produce, as Ares does, for vehicles that are `Cloneable` and match the structure's naval setting. Structures that leave the key out are unchanged.
