---
title: Show a defence's range ring when it is selected
category: feature
release: 0.2.0
targets:
- type: key
  id: ShowDefenceRange
  effect: changed
credit: [MentalHomiega]
---

A selected defence draws a ring at the range of its primary weapon. Your own defences draw the ring only while they have power. The Show defence range when selected checkbox in the game controls dialog and on the classic options page turns it on and off. It is on by default in the Modern interface and off in the Classic one. A structure whose type has its own radial indicator, such as Gap Generator or Nano Sensor, keeps that indicator and draws no second ring.
