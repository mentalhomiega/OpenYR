---
title: Board a friendly transport with a Crazy Ivan instead of bombing it
category: fix
release: 0.2.0
targets:
- type: system
  id: ivan-bombs
  effect: changed
credit: [MentalHomiega]
---

A click with an `Ivan=yes` soldier on a friendly transport with room now orders it to board, as in Yuri's Revenge, instead of planting a bomb on the transport. A full transport gets the no-enter cursor. A friendly vehicle that is not a transport can still be bombed.
