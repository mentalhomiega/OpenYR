---
title: Aim a superweapon at a trigger's offensive or defensive cell (SW.AITargeting.Preference)
category: feature
release: 0.2.0
targets:
- type: key
  id: SW.AITargeting.Preference
  effect: added
credit: [MentalHomiega]
---

`SW.AITargeting.Preference` makes the computer aim a superweapon at the offensive or defensive cell a trigger set, or ignore both, as Ares does. Without the key, a weapon whose `SW.AITargeting` is `Nuke`, `MultiMissile`, `LightningStorm` or `ParaDrop` now aims at the offensive cell while one is set, as the stock nuke and paradrops already did. Other weapons aim as before.
