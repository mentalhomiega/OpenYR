A giant squid now grapples its victim as in Yuri's Revenge. It draws the `SQDG` animation around the victim, rocks the victim left and right, and bites every 40 frames with three splashes and three wakes around the victim. Before, the squid bit like a terror drone. A parasite that comes out is now paralyzed for three times its `ROF`, not one.
