category: fix

A map whose playable area is narrower than the screen is now centered horizontally and no longer scrolls sideways. A map shorter than the screen is centered vertically and no longer scrolls up and down. Black fills the screen around the map. Such a map used to flicker and slide around as the view scrolled.
