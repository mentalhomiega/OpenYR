The modern interface frames its panels in blue, red or purple for the three stock sides. A side after the third now takes the hue of its first country's color scheme, so Mental Omega's Foehn gets a teal panel, and a side's section can set the color with [`Hud.Color=`](/keys/hud.color/). The Allied button icons are drawn in the new hue. The three stock sides look as before.
