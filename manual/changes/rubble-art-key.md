A structure with `LeaveRubble=yes` can now name its ruins in its art entry with `Rubble=`, as in Yuri's Revenge. The shape's first frame is drawn across the footprint, with no shadow. A structure without the key keeps leaving frame 3 of its own image, so stock structures look as before.
