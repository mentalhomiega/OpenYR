The free color picker in the skirmish and network lobbies now writes the chosen color as red, green and blue values with the hex code and as hue, saturation and brightness, names the closest of the eight lobby colors, and shows the sixteen steps of the remap ramp that units are drawn from. A player who cannot tell the swatch from its neighbors can read the choice.
