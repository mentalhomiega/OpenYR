---
title: Let a missile super weapon set its own take-off animation (Nuke.TakeOff)
category: feature
release: 0.2.0
targets:
- type: key
  id: Nuke.TakeOff
  effect: added
credit: [MentalHomiega]
---

A silo launching a super weapon with `Nuke.TakeOff` now plays that animation in place of `[General] NukeTakeOff`, as Ares does. Super weapons that leave the key out are unchanged.
