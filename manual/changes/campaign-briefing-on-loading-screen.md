A campaign mission that prints its briefing on the loading screen no longer opens on a separate objectives screen, as in Yuri's Revenge. Before, the screen followed the loading screen, repeating the briefing. The screen stays available from the game options, and a mission that prints no briefing on its loading screen still shows it at the start.
