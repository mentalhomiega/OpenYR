A hit on an object under the Iron Curtain or a Force Shield now lights up the map around it, as in Yuri's Revenge: white for the Iron Curtain and blue for a Force Shield, about as big as a blast of twice the damage. Before, the hit made no flash. Healing and forced damage still pass through without a flash.
