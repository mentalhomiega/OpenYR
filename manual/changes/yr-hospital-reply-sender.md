---
title: Answer the infantry at a hospital or armory by its own dock
category: fix
release: 0.2.0
targets:
- type: key
  id: NumberOfDocks
  effect: changed
credit: [MentalHomiega]
---

A hospital or armory with several docks now answers a docking infantry by the dock that infantry holds, and leaves the infantry on the first dock alone while another dock is free. Before, every reply went to the first dock. A structure with one dock behaves as before.
