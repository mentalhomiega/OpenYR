title: Call airstrike planes when the designator shot detonates
category: fix

targets:
- type: key
  id: Airstrike
  effect: changed
credit: [MentalHomiega]
---

An airstrike weapon now fires its shot like any other, and its firer calls the planes when the shot detonates on the object it was fired at, as in Yuri's Revenge. Before, the planes were called at the moment of firing, and the shot was not created.
