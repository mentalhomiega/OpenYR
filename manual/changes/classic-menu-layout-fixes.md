In the classic menu style, the main menu's Exit Game button no longer sits over the menu's title. Options moves up under Movies & Credits, and Exit Game takes the foot of the right-hand column.

Options and then Keyboard opens inside the classic frame at 800x600. Before, the panel ran past the right edge, so the Category field and the Secondary column were cut off. Now the command list scrolls inside the panel, the editor stands at its right, Back and OK sit in the right-hand column, and Reset to defaults sits at the foot of the panel.

On the classic Options page, "Stretch videos to fill the screen" and "Use the Windows pointer" each sit on one line. Before, they wrapped into the line below.

The CnCNet lobby fits the classic stage. Before, its panel was 900 dp wide and ran past both sides at 800x600.

The Skirmish setup panel in the classic style starts below the title art. Before, its top edge covered the lower part of the Yuri's Revenge logo.

The modern menus are unchanged.
