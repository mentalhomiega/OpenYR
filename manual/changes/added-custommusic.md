---
title: Play the player's own music from a Music folder
category: feature
release: 0.2.0
targets:
- type: key
  id: CustomMusic
  effect: added
credit: [MentalHomiega]
---

Audio files (WAV, OGG, FLAC and MP3) in the `Music` folder of the user directory join the music list, titled with their file names. `CustomMusic=` under `[Audio]` in `RA2MD.INI`, and a Custom music list on the sound options screen, choose between the stock tracks, the player's own, or both. With an empty folder the music is unchanged.
