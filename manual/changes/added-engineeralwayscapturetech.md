---
title: Let tech structures follow the engineer capture level (EngineerAlwaysCaptureTech)
category: feature
release: 0.2.0
targets:
- type: key
  id: EngineerAlwaysCaptureTech
  effect: added
credit: [MentalHomiega]
---

A player's engineer now shows the enter cursor over a tech structure whatever its strength, as Ares does, unless `[General]` sets `EngineerAlwaysCaptureTech=no`. Rules that leave `EngineerCaptureLevel` at its default of 1 are unchanged.
