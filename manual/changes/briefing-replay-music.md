title: Pause the music while the briefing video replays
category: fix
release: 0.2.0
targets:
- type: system
  id: music
  effect: changed
credit: [ZivDero]

Replaying the briefing video during a mission now pauses the music track and resumes it where it stopped. The track used to start over from its beginning, and a track queued or silenced before the replay was lost.
