A projectile whose warhead is a nuke maker, mind control, disguise, Ivan bomb, electric assault, locomotor, bomb disarm, parasite, temporal or airstrike warhead now throws no shrapnel, as in Yuri's Revenge. Before, it threw its shrapnel as well as taking its warhead's effect.
