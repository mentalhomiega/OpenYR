`Culling=yes` is now read on warheads. A giant squid whose `Parasite=yes` warhead has it sinks a victim in the red health band at its next bite, and a victim in the yellow band when the squid is elite, instead of damaging it. Stock rules set it on `ParasitePlus`, so a squid now finishes a weakened ship.
