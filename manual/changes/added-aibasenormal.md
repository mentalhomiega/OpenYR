---
title: Keep a structure out of the AI base space
category: feature
release: 0.2.0
targets:
- type: key
  id: AIBaseNormal
  effect: added
credit: [MentalHomiega]
---

`AIBaseNormal=` sets whether a structure extends the base space its owner's computer-controlled buildings are placed in. Without it a structure extends the space unless it is a deployed vehicle other than a construction yard.
