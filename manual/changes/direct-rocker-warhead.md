---
title: Rock a vehicle that a direct rocker blast strikes
category: feature
release: 0.2.0
targets:
- type: key
  id: DirectRocker
  scope: warheadtype
  effect: added
credit: [MentalHomiega]
---

In a warhead's section of `rules.ini`, `DirectRocker=yes` makes a blast that strikes a vehicle rock it instead of damaging it. That blast deals no damage to the vehicle or to anything near it, and it throws no shrapnel. A blast that strikes infantry, a structure or an aircraft works as it does without the key.
