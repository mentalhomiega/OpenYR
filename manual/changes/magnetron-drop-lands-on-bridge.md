A vehicle that a Magnetron lets go above a bridge now lands on the deck, standing on it as a vehicle that drove there does, and it crushes the soldiers on the deck rather than those under the bridge. Before, it landed at ground height under the deck, and it crushed the soldiers under the bridge. A vehicle let go below the deck, or beside the bridge, lands on the ground as before.
