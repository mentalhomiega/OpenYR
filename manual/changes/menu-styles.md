The menus now show Yuri's Revenge's title art and colors in place of Tiberian Sun's. By default, the art fills the screen behind centered purple panels sized for 1920 by 1080 and 3840 by 2160 screens. [`MenuStyle=Classic`](/keys/menustyle/), or the switch on the display options screen, shows Yuri's Revenge's own menu screen instead. The main menu shows the version in its bottom right corner in both styles.
