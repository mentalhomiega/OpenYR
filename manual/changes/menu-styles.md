The menus now show Yuri's Revenge's title art and colors in place of Tiberian Sun's, and the title screen menus follow Yuri's Revenge: Single Player (New Campaign, Load Saved Game, Skirmish), Network, Movies & Credits (Sneak Peeks, View Credits), Options and Exit Game, named from the game's string table. By default, the art fills the screen, the title screen menus and the options menu stand in one panel on the left, and other screens are centered purple panels sized for 1920 by 1080 and 3840 by 2160 screens. [`MenuStyle=Classic`](/keys/menustyle/), or the switch on the display options screen, shows Yuri's Revenge's own menu screen instead, with the buttons in its right-hand column. The main menu shows the version in a bottom corner in both styles.
