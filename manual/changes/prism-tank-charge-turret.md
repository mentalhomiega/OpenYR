---
title: Step the Prism Tank's turret through its stages as it rearms
category: feature
release: 0.2.0
targets:
- type: key
  id: IsChargeTurret
  effect: added
credit: [MentalHomiega]
---

A Prism Tank now shows a different turret stage as it rearms, and returns to its first turret once ready. Before, its turret stayed on the first stage. The change applies to any vehicle with `IsChargeTurret=yes`.
