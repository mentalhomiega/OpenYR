The **GPU voxels** group of the Graphics page sets [`GpuVoxels`](/keys/gpuvoxels/), [`GpuVoxelSmooth`](/keys/gpuvoxelsmooth/) and [`GpuVoxelScale`](/keys/gpuvoxelscale/). The rows are greyed until GPU drawing is ticked. The keys, their defaults and their file format are unchanged.
