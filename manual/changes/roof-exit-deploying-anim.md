A war factory shows the shape named by [`RoofDeployingAnim`](/keys/roofdeployinganim/) while a jumpjet or balloon-hover unit leaves through the roof. Before, it showed its [`DeployingAnim`](/keys/deployinganim/) front door art for that exit, so a Kirov Airship or Floating Disc left with the door open. Units that drive out still show `DeployingAnim`.
