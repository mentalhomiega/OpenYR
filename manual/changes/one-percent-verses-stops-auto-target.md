---
title: Stop choosing targets on their own at 1% Verses
category: fix
release: 0.2.0
targets:
- type: key
  id: Verses
  effect: changed
credit:
- MentalHomiega
---

A weapon whose warhead has `1%` against a target's armor no longer picks that target on its own, as in Yuri's Revenge. Before, only `0%` stopped it, so stock units with a `1%` entry against vehicles, such as the Virus, chose tanks. Ordered fire and retaliation at `1%` are unchanged.
