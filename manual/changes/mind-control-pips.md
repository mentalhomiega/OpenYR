title: Show a mind controller's captives as pips

- type: key
  id: PipScale

credit:

`PipScale=MindControl` shows one pip for each unit a selected mind controller may hold, lit for each one it holds, as in Yuri's Revenge. A controller with `InfiniteMindControl=yes` that holds more than its limit shows a red pip after the row, white as the overload strikes.
