---
title: Order every soldier to cheer
category: feature
release: 0.2.0
targets:
- type: command
  id: AllToCheer
  effect: added
credit: [MentalHomiega]
---

The AllToCheer command makes every idle soldier of the player's house play its cheer sequence and the house's cheer sound, as the stock command does. A soldier that is busy goes on with what it was doing.
