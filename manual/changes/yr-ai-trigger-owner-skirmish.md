---
title: Keep a country's AI triggers to that country in skirmish games
category: fix
release: 0.2.0
targets:
- type: system
  id: ai-team-production
  effect: changed
credit: [MentalHomiega]
---

An AI trigger that names a country as its owner is now drawn only by a computer house of that country in skirmish and multiplayer games, as in campaign games and as in Yuri's Revenge. Before, every computer house drew every enabled trigger. A Soviet house could then raise another country's teams, such as Iraq's Desolator team, which it could never fill, and its credits piled up unspent.
