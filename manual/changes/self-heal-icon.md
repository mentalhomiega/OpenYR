A selected soldier or organic vehicle now shows a cross beside its health bar while its owner has a structure with `InfantryGainSelfHeal`, and another vehicle shows a spanner while its owner has one with `UnitsGainSelfHeal`. The icon blinks while the object is hurt, as in Yuri's Revenge.
