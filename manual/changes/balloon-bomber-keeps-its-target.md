---
title: Keep bombing a target with a balloon bomber
category: fix
release: 0.2.0
targets:
- type: key
  id: BalloonHover
  effect: changed
credit: [MentalHomiega]
---

A BalloonHover unit whose weapon drops vertical bombs, such as the Kirov, now flies onto its target and keeps it until the target is destroyed, as in Yuri's Revenge. Before, it ran the general search for a firing spot that other units use.
