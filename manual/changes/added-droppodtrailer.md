---
title: Let rules set the smoke trail of falling drop pods (DropPodTrailer)
category: feature
release: 0.2.0
targets:
- type: key
  id: DropPodTrailer
  effect: added
credit: [MentalHomiega]
---

With `[General] DropPodTrailer` set, a falling drop pod leaves that animation in place of `SMOKEY`, also without a `DropPodWeapon`, as Ares does. Rules that leave the key out are unchanged.
