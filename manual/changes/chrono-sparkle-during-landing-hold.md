[`ChronoSparkle1`](/keys/chronosparkle1/) now plays every 24 frames over an object that a chrono teleport holds, as it does over one a temporal weapon is warping, as in Yuri's Revenge. Before, a Chrono Legionnaire or a chronosphere unit showed no sparkle while it waited to jump or held at its landing. An aircraft or structure takes the sparkle on its own position, and a garrisoned structure plays one at each occupant's muzzle flash point.
