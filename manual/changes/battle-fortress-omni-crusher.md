---
title: Flatten non-crushable objects with OmniCrusher
category: feature
release: 0.2.0
targets:
- type: key
  id: OmniCrusher
  effect: added
- type: key
  id: OmniCrushResistant
  effect: added
credit: [MentalHomiega]
---

An `OmniCrusher=yes` vehicle, such as the Battle Fortress, now flattens an enemy Tanya or Chrono Legionnaire that it runs over, though those types are not `Crushable`. It also drives onto its target when the target is out of weapon range. `OmniCrushResistant=yes` keeps a type from being flattened this way.
