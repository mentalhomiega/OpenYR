In the classic menu style, Options on the main menu now opens one page laid out like Yuri's Revenge's Options dialog, in place of the separate Game Settings, Display and Sound screens. It has visual details and the resolution list, difficulty, the tooltips, target lines and hidden objects switches, the scroll rate, and the music, sound and voice volumes. The Keyboard and Mods buttons open their screens from the right-hand column, where a More Options panel keeps the settings the original dialog does not have. OK applies the page, and Cancel applies nothing. The modern menu style and the in-game options menu are unchanged.
