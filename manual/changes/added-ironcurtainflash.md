---
title: Let rules turn off the flash on Iron Curtained objects (IronCurtainFlash)
category: feature
release: 0.2.0
targets:
- type: key
  id: IronCurtainFlash
  effect: added
credit: [MentalHomiega]
---

With `[AudioVisual] IronCurtainFlash=no`, a hit on an Iron Curtained or force-shielded object no longer makes a flash over it, as in Ares. Rules that leave the key out are unchanged.
