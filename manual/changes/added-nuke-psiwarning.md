---
title: Let a missile super weapon set or drop its target warning (Nuke.PsiWarning)
category: feature
release: 0.2.0
targets:
- type: key
  id: Nuke.PsiWarning
  effect: added
credit: [MentalHomiega]
---

A super weapon with `Nuke.PsiWarning` now marks its missile's target with that animation in place of `PSIWARN`, or with none for `none`, as Ares does. Super weapons that leave the key out are unchanged.
