---
title: Hide the repair wrench from enemies (EnemyWrench)
category: feature
release: 0.2.0
targets:
- type: key
  id: EnemyWrench
  effect: added
credit: [MentalHomiega]
---

With `[General] EnemyWrench=no`, players not allied with a structure's owner no longer see the repair wrench over it, as Ares does; observers still see it. Rules that leave the key out are unchanged.
