With the modern [menu style](/keys/menustyle/), the title menus stand in a solid panel with an accent edge on its left, larger left-aligned buttons, and a shade that darkens the menu battle behind the panel. The Main Menu has no caption and shows the game's logo above the panel while the menu battle runs; the logo is cut from the installed title picture and is left out when a mod replaces that picture. The player's name now shows at the top right on every title menu, and the version at the bottom left.
