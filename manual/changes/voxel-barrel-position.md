title: Seat the barrels of voxel-turret structures on their bases
category: fix
release: 0.2.0
targets: []
credit: [MentalHomiega]
---

The barrel of a Gattling cannon, SAM site, flak cannon and the other structures with `TurretAnimIsVoxel=yes` was drawn offset by its weapon's `FireFLH`, so it floated above and ahead of its base. It now sits on the base, as in Yuri's Revenge.
