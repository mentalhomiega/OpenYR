title: Match Yuri's Revenge Terror Drone behavior
category: fix
release: 0.2.0
targets:
- type: system
  id: parasites
  effect: changed
credit: [MentalHomiega]
---

A vehicle with a Terror Drone inside no longer fires back at the drone, so it stops shooting at the spot the drone leapt from. A drone whose victim dies comes out where the victim stood, not near its own starting point, and goes on to guard the area. A service depot repair step kills the drone, two drones no longer leap at one vehicle together, and a drone does not leap at an Iron Curtained target. A vehicle with a drone inside cannot board a transport or deploy into a structure, a grinder refunds the drone too, and a chronosphere warp no longer kills the drone.
