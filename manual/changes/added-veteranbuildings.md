title: Let a country start structures as veterans (VeteranBuildings)

- type: key
  id: VeteranBuildings
  effect: added

A country's `VeteranBuildings` list now makes its new structures of those types veterans and shows their alternate cameo in the player's sidebar, as Ares does. Countries that leave the key out are unchanged.
