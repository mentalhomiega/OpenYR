Superweapon sections read the generic Ares keys: `SW.*`, `Money.Amount`, `EVA.*`, `Message.*`, `Text.*`, `Cursor` and `NoCursor`, and the keys of the new behaviors. `Type=GenericWarhead`, `UnitDelivery`, `SonarPulse` and `Battery` are known behaviors. `Action=Custom` aims with the cursor a weapon names, and `[MouseCursors]` defines named cursors. A weapon with an unknown `Type=` stays charged and fires nothing.

The cameo that was clicked now fires its own weapon when two sections share an `Action=`. A silo launches the payload of the weapon that ordered the launch, whatever the order of `[SuperWeaponTypes]`.
