`DeployFacing` on a structure sets the direction a vehicle turns to before it deploys into it, and the direction the vehicle faces when the structure packs up, as in Yuri's Revenge. The Slave Miner now turns north before it deploys, as the stock rules ask. Structures that leave the key out still face south.
