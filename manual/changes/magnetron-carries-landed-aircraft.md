An aircraft the Magnetron lifts from the ground, such as a Harrier resting on its Air Force Command pad, now flies to the free spot beside the Magnetron and is let go there, like a lifted vehicle. Before, it rose and hung in the air where it stood. The pad is free as soon as the beam takes the aircraft. An aircraft the beam holds that is destroyed is removed where it hangs and does not stay in the air with no strength. One let go above its own pad lands on the structure and explodes, as a vehicle does.
