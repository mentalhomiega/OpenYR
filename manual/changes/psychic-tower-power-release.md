title: Release mind-controlled units when their structure loses power
category: fix
release: 0.2.0
targets:
- type: system
  id: mind-control
  effect: changed
credit: [MentalHomiega]
---

A structure that drops out of service for lack of power now releases the units it holds, as Yuri's Revenge does. A Psychic Tower whose house falls short of power frees its Gunners at once, and each one returns to the house it was taken from. Before, they stayed under the tower's control after its power failed.
