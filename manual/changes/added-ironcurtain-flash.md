---
title: Let a warhead decide whether its hits on Iron Curtained objects flash (IronCurtain.Flash)
category: feature
release: 0.2.0
targets:
- type: key
  id: IronCurtain.Flash
  effect: added
credit: [MentalHomiega]
---

A warhead with `IronCurtain.Flash` now decides whether its hits on an Iron Curtained or force-shielded object make a flash, in place of `[AudioVisual] IronCurtainFlash`, as Ares does. Warheads that leave the key out are unchanged.
