---
title: Pack a sold deployed structure back into its vehicle
category: fix
release: 0.2.0
targets:
- type: key
  id: UndeploysInto
  effect: changed
credit: [MentalHomiega]
---

Selling any structure that names an `UndeploysInto` vehicle, other than a construction yard, now turns it back into that vehicle at once, as in Yuri's Revenge. A deployed Slave Miner used to be sold for its refund unless it had a move order.
