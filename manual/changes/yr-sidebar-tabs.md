The sidebar now has Yuri's Revenge's layout: repair and sell buttons, four tabs, and the shown tab's cameos two to a row. Structures with `BuildCat=Combat` and superweapons share the second tab, other structures take the first, infantry the third, and vehicles and aircraft the fourth. The power and waypoint buttons are gone; their commands still work. Every scroll command now moves the shown tab's strip.
