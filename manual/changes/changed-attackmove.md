---
title: Hold the attack-move key for repeated orders
category: feature
release: 0.2.0
targets:
- type: command
  id: AttackMove
  effect: changed
credit:
- MentalHomiega
---

Tapping the attack-move key still makes the next click on the ground an attack-move order. Holding the key now makes every click on the ground an attack-move order until the key is released.
