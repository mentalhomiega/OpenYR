---
title: Choose who sees a superweapon timer (SW.TimerVisibility)
category: feature
release: 0.2.0
targets:
- type: key
  id: SW.TimerVisibility
  effect: added
credit: [MentalHomiega]
---

A superweapon with `ShowTimer=yes` can limit its countdown to its owner, its allies, its team or its enemies with `SW.TimerVisibility`, as Ares does. Weapons that leave the key out still show their timer to every player.
