---
title: Let a vehicle's turret show how many passengers it carries (PassengerTurret)
category: feature
release: 0.2.0
targets:
- type: key
  id: PassengerTurret
  effect: added
credit: [MentalHomiega]
---

A multi-turret vehicle with `PassengerTurret=yes` now shows the turret numbered by its passenger count, as Ares does, and its last turret when it carries more. Types that leave the key out are unchanged.
