With [Debug mode](/keys/debugmode/) on, five more commands join the Debug category. Full Power and Invulnerable Units are toggles. Win Mission and Lose Mission end the mission as a win or a loss through the path a scenario's win or lose action takes, and Charge Superweapons makes every superweapon the player has ready.
