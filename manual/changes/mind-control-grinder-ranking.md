A computer house now sends a unit it has taken over to the grinder or bio reactor that Yuri's Revenge picks. The distance runs from the centre of the unit to the centre of the structure, with no allowance for the structure's size, so a large structure no longer looks nearer than a small one at the same distance. A tie goes to the structure that was made later. Before, ties went to the earlier one.
