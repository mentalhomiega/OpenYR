A Rocketeer, Floating Disc or Cosmonaut that a map places without orders now stands on the ground until it is given a destination, as in Yuri's Revenge. Before, it rose into the air at once, where the shroud lifting off its cell did not reveal it. The two neutral Rocketeers in Allied mission 2 now join the player when their area is uncovered.
