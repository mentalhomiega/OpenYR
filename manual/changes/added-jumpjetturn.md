A jumpjet soldier with [`JumpJetTurn=yes`](/keys/jumpjetturn/#scope-infantrytype) turns toward its target while it hovers and holds its fire while it flies, as in Yuri's Revenge. Before, the key was ignored and every jumpjet soldier was drawn facing its target.
