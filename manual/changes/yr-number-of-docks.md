A structure now holds one radio contact per `NumberOfDocks=`, as in Yuri's Revenge, and an aircraft that docks heads for its own dock's `DockingOffsetN=` spot. The Air Force Command takes four Harriers instead of one, and its pad rearms all of them. Saved games from earlier builds do not load.
