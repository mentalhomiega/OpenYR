---
title: Add ShadowStacking for darkening overlapping shadows once
category: feature
release: 0.2.0
targets:
- type: key
  id: ShadowStacking
  effect: added
credit:
- MentalHomiega
---

[`ShadowStacking=no`](/keys/shadowstacking/) makes overlapping vehicle and aircraft shadows darken the ground once instead of again under each, so a crowd of aircraft no longer turns the ground black. It is on by default, which keeps the original game's look.
