---
title: Set or turn off a warhead's nuclear flash (NukeFlash.Duration)
category: feature
release: 0.2.0
targets:
- type: key
  id: NukeFlash.Duration
  effect: added
credit: [MentalHomiega]
---

`NukeFlash.Duration` on a warhead sets how many frames the map-wide nuclear flash lasts when its projectile explodes, as Ares does. Any warhead can now flash, and `0` turns the flash of the `NUKE` warhead off. Warheads that leave the key out are unchanged.
