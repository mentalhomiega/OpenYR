## Compatibility notes

- The `Missions` name table stays in `code/_mission.cpp`, and `MissionType` stays in `code/mission.hh`. The manual's enum drift check reads both files, so moving the table would change that contract.
- The numbering is pinned, not changed: all 32 numbers, including `MISSION_EATEN` (9), `MISSION_WAIT` (28) and `MISSION_ATTACK_MOVE` (29), and every name, including `Area Guard`, `Selling` and `Attack Move`.
- The name comparison was `stricmp`; the header folds ASCII letters only. They differ only for bytes above 127 under a non-"C" locale, and the engine never sets a locale.
- `MissionControlClass` members and `Serialize` are untouched, so saves are unchanged.

## Not tested, and why

- `MissionClass::Mission_From_Name` and `Mission_Name` themselves, `MissionControlClass::Read_INI` and `Is_Recruitable_Mission`: they need `CCINIClass`, the `MissionControl` global and the object base classes. The rules they apply are tested through the header; the INI reads and the global table are not.
- `MissionType_From_Name` and `MissionType_To_Name` are declared in `mission.hh` but defined nowhere in `code/`, so there was nothing to test.
- Rules defaults outside missions: they are spread over the `RulesClass` constructor and `Read_INI` functions that read through `CCINIClass`, so extracting them would be a larger change than this job.
- Whether `Missions` is bounds-checked: `To_Name` and `Is_Recruitable_Mission` index with the caller's value, as before.
