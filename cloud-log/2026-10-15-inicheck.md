## Findings while reading

- The `Tile%02d` keys are not read from the `TileSet%04d` section. `code/isotype.cpp` (lines 1088 to 1102) reads them from the section that the set's `SetName=` value names, and only when that section exists. The 2026-10-06 export put them under `@tile-set`, which would have made the checker accept them in the wrong section. They are now `@tile-set-name`, and `Tile01Anim=` inside a `TileSet` section is reported.
- The engine reads tile sets from `TileSet0000` up and stops at the first section whose `TilesInSet` is missing or negative (`code/isotype.cpp` around line 925). The checker stops at the same place; later sets and their `SetName` sections show under `--unchecked`.
- `code/infatype.cpp` line 348 declares `char facing[10]` and line 353 scans it with an unbounded `%s` from a do-string of up to 31 characters. A facing token of 10 or more characters overflows the stack buffer. Suggested fix: scan with `%9s`. I did not change engine code. The checker reports any facing that is not one of the eight names.
- `Read_Sequence_INI` ignores an unknown or lower-case facing without a message, and reads the numbers with `%d`, so `$10`, `1 ,2,3` and `1,2.5,3` are not read as the writer meant. The checker reports these.
- The do-string names `FireUp`, `FireProne`, `SecondaryFire` and `SecondaryProne` are also art keys holding a launch frame (`code/infatype.cpp` lines 436 to 440). The catalog now has both readings; each applies only in its own section.
- `manual/data/ini-keys.yaml` on `modern` is behind the source: `manage.py update` adds 153 lines (for example `BarrelCompressFrames`). The checker would report those keys as unread until the file is regenerated and committed. I discarded the churn.
