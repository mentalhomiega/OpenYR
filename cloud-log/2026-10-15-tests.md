## Compatibility notes

- Behaviour is preserved in every move. The expressions keep their types, so the casts, the single-precision truncations and the order of steps are the same as before. The test target for each header is registered in `tests/CMakeLists.txt` in the commit that adds the test.
- `CELL_SPREAD_MAX` moved from `code/combat.h` to `code/cellspread.h`, which `code/combat.h` includes. Every file that read it still sees it.
- No saved field, rules key or manual page changed, so `code/savever.h` and `manual/` are untouched.
- A mutation check confirmed the production test catches a missing single-precision cut: with the cut removed, 4 of its 44 checks fail.

## What the tests pin

- Cell spread: the 12 counts, the order of ring 1 and the start of ring 2, rings 1 to 10 holding exactly the cells whose longer offset plus half the shorter (rounded down) equals the ring number, no repeats before ring 11, mirror symmetry of rings 0 to 10, and the ring 11 differences from that rule (60 entries, 59 different cells, `(-3,11)` twice, `(3,-11)` and `(+-2,+-10)`, `(+-3,+-10)` absent from the whole table). Also the radius a `CellSpread` selects and its clamp.
- Veterancy: the experience a kill adds, the float32 store (three kills of a third reach exactly 1), the cap, the fall back to the cap on a kill worth nothing, a zero cost or ratio reaching the cap, the victim value (1, 2, 3 times the cost, 0 for an ally at any rank), and the truncating multiply and divide (`VeteranCombat=1.1` raises 100 to 110, not 210).
- Prone damage: truncation to a whole number (15 at 0.5 deals 7) and the one-point minimum.
- Low power: defaults of 0.5, 0.9 and 1, each limit, the penalty modifier, the order of minimum then maximum, the float32 0.01 floor, truncation of the power ratio (35 frames at 7/12 power become 60; without the cut they become 59), and a ratio a hair under 1 counting as short.
- Purifier: payout in credits and points, no rounding of the bonus to whole units (a half unit still pays), truncation of `PurifierBonus` and the share to single precision (`PurifierBonus=0.1` on 10 ore pays 24 credits and 4 points), and every no-payout case.
- `HarvestersPerRefinery`: the list lookup (a slot past the end is 0), the product with the refineries owned, and the `AISlaveMinerNumber` fallback when no refinery type is buildable.

## Questions for MentalHomiega

- `VeteranCombat`, `VeteranSpeed`, `VeteranSight`, `VeteranArmor`, `VeteranROF` and `ProneDamage` are read into doubles. Commit c0387089 truncated `MaxLowPowerProductionSpeed` and `MultipleFactory` to single precision because gamemd stores them as float32, but not `MinLowPowerProductionSpeed` or `LowPowerPenaltyModifier`. If gamemd stores the veteran bonuses and `ProneDamage` as float32 too, the values would differ: `1.1` truncated to single is 1.0999999, so `VeteranCombat=1.1` would raise 100 to 109, not 110. Please confirm which of these gamemd keeps as float32. The tests pin the double behaviour that is in the code now.
- Does `Experience_After_Kill` read `VeteranRatio` and `VeteranCap` as float32 in gamemd? The total is stored as a float, but the divisor is computed here in double.

## Not tested, and why

- `VeterancyClass` rank thresholds and setters: they were not added since `1cb8bf7`, and `code/veteran.cpp` pulls in `rules.h` for `Made_A_Kill`. The arithmetic of `Made_A_Kill` is tested through the header.
- Which object earns a kill (transport, launcher, garrison occupant) in `Record_The_Kill`: it needs `TechnoClass` state. Only the value the credit carries is tested.
- The order of the steps in `Take_Damage`, `Fire_Damage` and `Current_Speed`: each rank step is tested alone; the steps around it need the object.
- `Modify_Damage` falloff by `CellSpread` and `PercentAtMax`, and the object walk in `Explosion_Damage`: they read the warhead, the scenario flags and the map. The falloff is a candidate for the next lift, since it takes only the damage, `CellSpread`, `PercentAtMax`, the distance and the armor factor.
- The count of purifiers in `HouseClass::Purified` (buildings in limbo, destroyed ones still on the map, `AIVirtualPurifiers`): it loops over `Buildings` and reads the session type. The payout is tested from the count.
- `Count_Resource_Gatherers`, `Count_Owned` and the `gatherers < target` test in `AI_Unit`: they need the house's object lists. The comparison is one `<` and stays in place.
- The country build-time multiplier, `BuildTimeMultiplier` and the `MultipleFactory` loop in `Time_To_Build`: only the power step was in scope.
- `BuildingClass::Power_Output` (commit bbbf7142, an exact whole result comes out one lower unless the ratio is exact in binary): not on the job list, but it lifts the same way and would be a good next test.
