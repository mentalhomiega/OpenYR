### INI checker

`IniCheck::Check_Rules` parses INI text the way `INIClass::Load` splits lines and keeps line numbers. It checks a section the catalog names, such as `[General]`, or one a rules list such as `[VehicleTypes]` names, and reports keys not read there, keys that differ from a read key only in case (the engine matches names exactly), and values `Get_Bool`, `Get_Int`, `Get_Float` or `Read_Numbers` would not read. Sections no list names, such as weapons, are skipped and listed with `--unchecked`. The catalog is a TSV exported from `manual/data/ini-keys.yaml` by `tools/inicheck/export_catalog.py`, because the engine has no YAML reader.

Known gaps for the next step: keys the engine builds with a pattern, such as `DockingOffset%d`, are not in the catalog and are reported as unread; weapons, projectiles and difficulty sections are not checked; `art.ini` image sections are not placed. Nothing calls the library from the game yet.
