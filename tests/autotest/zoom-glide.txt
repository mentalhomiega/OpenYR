; Zoom glide: ten mouse-wheel steps out from zoom 1 to the furthest zoom, then ten back in, three game frames apart, on XMP22S8.
; Each step that needs more of the map keeps the picture already drawn and draws only the new edges, and the presenter
; stretches the drawn picture while the zoom glides. The map's surfaces and its depth and alpha buffers are made once, at the
; first step, for the largest view: mapbuffers logs the zoom steps and how many of them made buffers. After each glide has
; settled, the view is shot as the frames left it and, a game frame later, after a full redraw. drawdigest 2 logs one
; DRAW_DIGEST line for each game frame whose draws all show the same picture: a second line for a frame means the shot's
; full redraw differs from the kept picture. perfwindow logs the frame times of each glide; the Run line's filter leaves
; those lines out, as they depend on the machine.
