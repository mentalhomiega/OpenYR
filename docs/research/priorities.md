# Priorities for Ares and Phobos support

This ranking is provisional. It rests on the Ares and Phobos documentation, on the tag tables in [`ares-tags.md`](ares-tags.md) and [`phobos-tags.md`](phobos-tags.md), and on the owner's scan of Mental Omega, which found that most keys the engine does not read are Ares tags. Run [`tools/mod_scan.py`](tools/mod_scan.py) again after each family lands to replace the guesses below with usage counts.

## Ranked plan

1. **Per-armor warhead rules (`Versus.<armor>` and its ForceFire, Retaliate and PassiveAcquire variants, Ares).** Mods that define extra armor types need these before their weapons behave sensibly, and the change stays inside warhead and armor lookup.
2. **Ares super weapon keys (`SW.*`, `EVA.*`, `Message.*`, `Money.*`, `Text.*`, `Light.*`, `ParaDrop.*` and the per-type keys such as `Lightning.*` and `Chronosphere.*`).** The Mental Omega scan lists these prefixes among the most used unknown keys. Most are parsed fields on `code/super.cpp` and `code/suprtype.cpp`; the AI targeting keys (`SW.AITargeting*`) are large and can follow the rest.
3. **Small per-type switches that only add a parsed field and one check.** Examples are the Ares veteran and elite ability names that mirror stock immunities (EMPIMMUNE, RADIMMUNE, POISONIMMUNE), and the Phobos weapon and warhead flags marked small in the tables. They are cheap, common in mod files, and raise the scan coverage figure quickly.
4. **Per-type copies of global rules (Phobos technos and buildings).** Keys such as `CurleyShuffle`, `HarvesterDumpRate`, `HarvesterLoadRate`, `OpenTopped.RangeBonus`, `OccupyDamageMultiplier`, `Units.RepairRate` and `Wake` override a global value, so each needs one new type field and a fallback to the global. The Phobos source confirms the fallback for the first five. Two global names differ from the per-type name: `OpenTopped.RangeBonus` falls back to the global `OpenToppedRangeBonus`, and `Units.RepairRate`, when set, replaces both `IRepairRate` and `URepairRate`.
5. **Ares building keys that mods use heavily (`Foundation.*` and `FoundationOutline.*` custom shapes, `UC.*` units passing through buildings, `SpyEffect.*`, `Academy.*`, `Rubble.*`, `PrismForwarding.*`).** The scan names `Foundation.*`, `UC.*` and `SpyEffect.*` among the top prefixes. Custom foundations touch placement, pathing and drawing and are large; the Ares page does not list the key spellings, so check them against a mod file first.
6. **Phobos shields (`ShieldType`, 104 rows).** A shield is a second health pool with its own armor, self-healing, respawn, animations and warhead interactions. Mental Omega's next major version is expected to rely on Phobos, and shields are self-contained: they need per-techno state in saves, damage hooks in `code/techno.cpp` and `code/combat.cpp`, and the pip drawing in `code/tactical.cpp`.
7. **Attached effects (Phobos `AttachEffectType`, 138 rows; Ares `AttachEffect.*`).** Many unit abilities in big mods are built from them. The engine already runs the Ares form in `code/attacheffect.cpp`; the Phobos form adds explicit types, discard conditions, cumulative instances, expiry weapons, reflected damage and weapon requirements, so it is large and needs per-techno state in saves. Plan it after the cheaper items, and reuse the existing multiplier code.
8. **Ares warhead and weapon extensions (`IronCurtain.*`, `IvanBomb.*`, `IonCannon.*`, `Culling.*`, `RelativeDamage.*`, `DisableWeapons.Duration`, `Flash.Duration`, `Wave.*`, `Bolt.*`, `Beam.*`).** Each adds one effect to an existing warhead or weapon path and the scan shows `IronCurtain.*` and `IvanBomb.*` in use.
9. **Veterancy, experience and bounty tags (Ares).** Economy and balance tuning in large mods leans on them, and the engine already tracks experience, so the change reuses existing state.
10. **Weapon selection and targeting controls on technos (Phobos `ForceWeapon.*`, `NoSecondaryWeaponFallback`, `MultiWeapon`, targeting delays).** Mods use them to make multi-weapon units pick sensible weapons, and they change only the weapon choice and scan timing in `code/techno.cpp`.
11. **Automatic death, type conversion and anim-to-unit (Phobos `AutoDeath.*`, `Convert.*`, `CreateUnit`).** Temporary units, deploy-to-transform units and wreck-to-unit effects depend on them; type conversion is shared with Ares and needs careful save handling, so it is medium to large.
12. **Projectile trajectories and interception (Phobos).** They unlock whole weapon families but add a new bullet-motion subsystem, so they rank below the tags that only read data.
13. **Building behaviour (Phobos grinder, factory plant, power plant enhancer, upgrade and placement tags).** Each is small to medium and self-contained in `code/building.cpp` and `code/builtype.cpp`, but fewer mods depend on any single one than on the techno items above.
14. **Ares side and country defaults (`ParaDrop.*`, `StartInMultiplayer.*`, score screen and loading screen keys, `AI.PowerPlants`, `AI.BaseDefenses`).** Small reads on `code/houstype.cpp` and `code/side.cpp`; the interface keys only matter once the front end uses them.
15. **AI scripts, trigger events and trigger actions (Ares).** Campaign and skirmish content rely on them, and they extend the numbered script system already present in `code/_script.cpp`.
16. **Spawner, hijacker, driver and harvester behaviour (Ares and Phobos).** Each is a distinct feature on specific unit types and can follow demand from the scan.
17. **Tiberium features (Ares `TiberiumHeal`, `TiberiumDamageEnabled`, `TiberiumExplosive`, `Refinery.UseStorage`), terrain and ore tags (Phobos), laser trails and custom radiation types.** Some Ares tiberium keys, such as `TiberiumHeal` and `TiberiumProof`, are already ticked. The Phobos laser trail and radiation types are large (new drawing code and per-site state) and matter to a few mods; terrain and ore keys are small.
18. **Animation drawing and damage tags (Phobos draw offsets, visibility, fire animations, animation damage).** Most are small art-side reads; they affect looks more than play, except the animation damage settings, which change balance and should move up if the scan shows them in use.
19. **Interface tags (Ares and Phobos sidebar, tooltips, digital displays, select boxes, hotkeys).** They affect presentation only, so they come after behaviour that changes gameplay; `[Phobos]` keys in RA2MD.INI are player settings and need a settings page rather than rules parsing.

## Not ranked

The Ares bug fix rows (143 pages) are listed in [`ares-tags.md`](ares-tags.md) but not ranked; they describe behaviour the engine may already have, and each needs a check against `code/` before it becomes work. The Phobos AI scripting and mapping page and the miscellaneous page (insignia types, debugging aids) are not tabled yet.
