# Small per-type and per-warhead switches (Ares and Phobos)

Sources: Ares documentation pages (December 2020 build) `new/manual`, `new/civilianenemies`, `new/canpassiveaquire`, `new/omnicrusher`, `new/passengers`, `new/prerequisites`, `new/turretrot`, `new/cloak/cloakstates`, `new/cloak/cloaksound`, `new/cloak/cloakstages`, `new/buildings/massselect`, `new/buildings/forceshield`, `new/warheads/berserk`, `ironcurtain`, `general`, `unitlost`, `diesound`, `deathweapon`, `flash`, `infantry` and `damageair`. Phobos (commit 71316fd) `docs/New-or-Enhanced-Logics.md` (sections "No Manual Move", "No rearm and reload in EMP or temporal", "Exclusion from base center calculations", "Disguise logic additions", "Range finding in cylinder", "CellSpread enhancement", "Warhead that can not kill", "Customize whether warhead can prevent crew escape from techno") and `docs/Fixed-or-Improved-Logics.md` ("Customizable Warhead trigger conditions"). Phobos source files read: `src/Ext/Techno/Hooks.cpp`, `Hooks.ReceiveDamage.cpp`, `Body.Update.cpp`, `Hooks.Firing.cpp`, `src/Ext/WarheadType/Body.cpp`, `Hooks.cpp`, `Detonate.cpp`, `src/Ext/House/Hooks.cpp`, `src/Ext/TechnoType/Body.cpp`, `src/Ext/Rules/Body.cpp`, `src/Utilities/EnumFunctions.cpp`.

This group holds the switches that add one parsed field and one condition at a place the engine already visits (priority 2 in `docs/research/priorities.md`). There are no Mental Omega usage counts yet, so the choice rests on how often mods of that kind use the key and on whether one existing function can carry the check. Run `docs/research/tools/mod_scan.py` on the mod and reorder the build list if the counts disagree.

Left out on purpose:

- The Ares veteran and elite abilities `EMPIMMUNE`, `RADIMMUNE`, `POISONIMMUNE`, `PSIONICSIMMUNE`, `PSIONICWEAPONIMMUNE` and `UNWARPABLE` are in the engine already (`code/ability.hh`, `TechnoClass::Is_Immune_To_EMP` and its siblings in `code/techno.cpp`).
- Keys with their own spec: `PROTECTED_DRIVER`, `ImmuneToAbduction`, `EMP.Modifier`, `EMP.Sparkles`, `EffectsRequireVerses`, the `Bounty.*` keys, `Promote.*`, `AttackNoThreatBuildings`, `NoAmmoAmount`.
- Keys whose effect needs more than one check: Phobos `RemoveDisguise` and the other per-target warhead effects (the engine has no per-target effect step after a detonation), `NoReload.*` (the engine has no general ammo reload timer; `UnitClass::Reload` serves `NoFireWhileMoving` units only), `Burst.NoDelay`, `ImmuneToCrit`, `DriverKilled.*`, `Carryall.*`, `HunterSeeker.Ignore`.

## Keys

The sections are `rules.ini` (`rulesmd.ini`) unless a row says otherwise. "Check" is the one condition the key adds. "Applies in" names the engine function that carries it; each name was checked with grep against `code/`. Where a row says "Ares", the behaviour comes from the Ares documentation alone and needs the comparison tests below before it counts as settled. "Phobos" rows were read from Phobos source.

## Behaviour

Only keys with an edge case are listed. Every other row behaves as its Check column says.

- `NoManualFire`: the key removes the player's attack cursor and order only. A unit with it still retaliates, auto-targets and takes team and script attack orders. Force-fire with the control key goes through the same `What_Action` result and is blocked too.
- `NoManualUnload`: the Ares page says passengers may still leave when the vehicle is destroyed. The existing `Resolve_Passengers_On_Death` rules decide that and need no change.
- `NoManualEnter`: scripts and computer houses can still load the object. `Passengers.Allowed` is the key that stops those.
- `NoManualMove` on a building: the unit-style move order and the rally-point click both reach `BuildingClass::Active_Click_With`. Phobos skips the undeploy part and the Phobos page says the rally point is still set; confirm with the real build (Open questions 8).
- `MassSelectable`: the engine today picks up a building when it has `UndeploysInto` and is neither a construction yard nor `MobileWar`, or when it is `SelectableCombatant`. Ares ties the default to a 1x1 foundation. The first build step keeps the engine rule as the default and adds the key as an override.
- `CivilianEnemy`: stock YR treats an object of a `MultiplayPassive=yes` house as no target in multiplayer unless the game option `AttackNeutralUnits` is on. Insignificant objects are skipped by a separate test. The Ares page does not say whether the key also lifts that test (Open questions 2).
- `CanPassiveAquire.Guard` and `.Cloak`: both need `CanPassiveAquire=yes` and apply to human-owned objects only. They act on the Guard mission only; Guard area and Hunt keep scanning. "Cloak-capable" means `Cloakable=yes`, a cloak crate or the `CLOAK` ability, which is the test the engine already uses.
- `OmniCrusher.Aggressive=no`: the unit still crushes anything it drives over on a move order. Only the approach to an attack target changes.
- `Passengers.Allowed` and `Passengers.Disallowed`: match by exact type. A type in both lists is refused (Open questions 6). Every route into a hold calls `Can_Fit_Passenger`, so the lists also bind computer houses and scripts.
- `Passengers.BySize=no`: the capacity test becomes "passenger count below `Passengers`". `SizeLimit` is still tested. The door-close test in `UnitClass::Receive_Message` (`RADIO_IM_IN`), the matching test in `AircraftClass::Receive_Message` and the selection pips read `CargoClass::Total_Size`, so they must use the same count.
- `Prerequisite.Negative`: `HouseClass::Can_Build` returns early for a type reached by `PrerequisiteOverride`, a secret lab or reverse engineering. The negative list is tested before those shortcuts. Computer houses return 1 before the prerequisite block, so they need their own test in `HouseClass::AI_Has_Prerequisites`; the Ares page does not say whether the AI obeys the list (Open questions 7).
- `IgnoreForBaseCenter`: Phobos applies it to buildings in the base center and to every techno in the start-position calculation. `HouseClass::Recalc_Center` only weighs buildings, so only that loop and `DisplayClass::Compute_Start_Pos` need the test.
- `NoRearm.*`: the countdown is held, not reset, so the remaining delay is still owed when the stun ends. The Phobos page says the airport reload of aircraft is unaffected; `Arm` is not the airport reload.
- `CylinderRangefinding`: Phobos skips the height difference when the key is on or the shooter is an aircraft. `TechnoClass::Fire_Origin` skips it for any airborne shooter. Keep the engine rule and add the key as a second reason to skip (Open questions 10).
- `Cloakable.Allowed=no`: the Ares page lists only self-cloak, cloak generators and crates. An attached effect that grants cloaking (`AttachEffect.Cloakable`) is not mentioned (Open questions 5).
- `CloakSound` and `DecloakSound`: with neither key set, both sounds equal the old single `CloakSound`, so existing mods behave as before.
- `Cloakable.Stages`: the fade state is saved as a stage number. The stage count itself is rules data, so a save loaded under rules with a different count is outside what the save format promises.
- `ImmuneToBerserk`: three states. Unset follows psionics immunity; `yes` and `no` override it. The key does not touch mind control, which keeps `Is_Immune_To_Psionics`.
- `Berserk.ROFMultiplier`: the engine halves `Rearm_Delay` with integer division. Use the multiplier and truncate, so the default `0.5` gives the same result for every non-negative delay.
- `IronCurtain.Modifier` and `ForceShield.Modifier`: only a positive duration is scaled. A result of 0 frames leaves the object unprotected and plays no effect. The Force Shield page says "seconds"; durations are frames (Open questions 9).
- `UseDisguiseMovementSpeed`: the engine's `DisguiseType` is an infantry type, so the key changes speed only for a disguised infantryman. A mirage-style terrain disguise has no speed.
- House filters (`AffectsEnemies`, `AffectsOwner`, `AffectsAllies`): the target's house is compared with the firer's house. The same house uses `AffectsOwner` (default: the `AffectsAllies` value). An allied house uses `AffectsAllies`. Any other house, a neutral house included, uses `AffectsEnemies`. `Is_Ally` is true for the firer's own house, so the existing `AffectsAllies` test must be split by comparing the houses first. With no `source`, the existing code skips the test; use `sourcehouse` when it is set.
- `AffectsNeutral`, `AffectsBelowPercent`, `AffectsAbovePercent`, `AffectsVeterancy`: Phobos tests them at the start of damage handling and zeroes the damage, so forced damage is blocked too. Health ratio must be above `AffectsAbovePercent` and at or below `AffectsBelowPercent`; a target with 0 health passes the lower bound. A value above 1 or ending in `%` is read as a percent.
- `AffectsAir` and `AffectsGround`: if both are `no`, the warhead hurts nothing. The Phobos page defines air as "above 208 leptons"; the code asks whether the object is airborne (Open questions 11).
- `DamageAirThreshold`: compare the blast height above the ground cell with the value. At the default `0` a blast on the ground hurts no flyer, as now.
- `CanKill=no`: the clamp applies only to damage that is not healing and only when the warhead affects the target's house. The target keeps 1 hit point even when it was at full health, and the next hit leaves it at 1 again. The result still counts as damage for armor and veterancy.
- `PreventCrewEscape`: the flag applies when the object is destroyed, not when it is hit. A vehicle with `Passengers` above 0 already produces no crew.
- `PreventPassengerEscape` and `PreventOccupantEscape`: the passengers or occupants die as kills credited to the warhead's firer. `BuildingClass::Kill_Occupants` dereferences its `assaulter` argument, so it needs a null guard for a warhead with no firer.
- `UnitLost.Suppress`: stops the line only for the object the warhead kills. The Ares page names units; whether a destroyed structure's line is also suppressed is open (Open questions 12).
- `DieSound.Override` and `VoiceDie.Override`: the stock pick uses `NonCriticalRandomNumber`, which is not synchronised, so the override needs no care for multiplayer.
- `Flash.Duration`: stock healing already flashes for 7 frames through `ObjectClass::Take_Damage`. The Ares page does not say whether the key replaces that.
- `Damage.Deployed`: applies to infantry for which `InfantryClass::Is_Deployed` is true. Unlike `ProneDamage`, the Ares page does not say whether forced damage is exempt.

## What stock YR does

- Players can attack, move and unload with any armed, mobile or loaded object. `EMPulseCannon` and `LimpetMine` buildings refuse manual attacks.
- Enter cursors show over every transport. A healer shows the guard-area cursor on itself.
- Auto-targeting skips objects of `MultiplayPassive` houses in multiplayer. A human-owned cloak-capable object on Guard takes no target on its own ([target selection](../../../manual/content/systems/target-selection.md)); other objects on Guard scan.
- An `OmniCrusher=yes` vehicle drives at its attack target to crush it.
- A passenger boards when the transport has room, allows its `Size` and is not a vehicle aboard a non-vehicle transport. There is no list by type.
- A type's `Prerequisite` and `PrerequisiteOverride` are the only build gates besides tech level and house. A drag-selection takes buildings that undeploy into a unit.
- Every object cloaks in the same number of stages and with the same sound. Berserk objects halve their reload time. The Iron Curtain and the Force Shield last for the rules' durations on every target. A turret turns at the body's `ROT`.
- A Psychedelic warhead sends non-allied vehicles, infantry and aircraft berserk unless the type is `ImmuneToPsionics`.
- With `AffectsAllies=no` a warhead spares the firer's own house and its allies; otherwise it hits every object in reach. Airborne objects are hit only when the blast is above the ground. A hit that would kill always kills. Destroyed vehicles, buildings and transports release crew and passengers by their own rules. Scatter, "unit lost" speech, death sounds and death weapons follow the victim, not the warhead. An object flashes when it is healed or clicked as a target.
- The rearm countdown runs while an object is stunned, deactivated or held by a temporal weapon. Range ignores height only for airborne shooters. Disguised infantry move at their own speed. The base center and start position count every building, and the start position counts every starting object.

## Where it hooks in OpenYR

The engine is a port of the game, so each key attaches to the function that already makes the decision. The table rows name them. Common work for every key:

- Read the key in `TechnoTypeClass::Read_INI`, `InfantryTypeClass::Read_INI`, `BuildingTypeClass::Read_INI`, `WarheadTypeClass::Read_INI`, `WeaponTypeClass::Read_INI` or the `RulesClass` function in the "Global keys" table; initialise it in the constructor with the default shown.
- Add it to the matching `Serialize` method, and to `Compute_CRC` where the key changes game state (see Saved state).
- Run `python manual/tools/manage.py update`, document the key on the owning manual page (`cloaking.md`, `transports.md`, `warheads.md`, `target-selection.md`, `production.md`, `disguises.md`, `emp-pulse.md`, `temporal-weapons.md`) and raise `SaveVersionInfo::REVISION` in `code/savever.h`.

What exists and helps:

- `AbilityType` and `TechnoClass::Has_Ability` carry the six immunity abilities; nothing to add for them.
- `TechnoClass::Take_Damage` already has the `AffectsAllies`, Psychedelic, Iron Curtain, scatter and death-sound code, and receives the warhead, the source and `sourcehouse`.
- `BuildingClass::IsSurvivorless` is saved and checksummed and stops building crew.
- `Explosion_Damage` builds one hit list and calls `Take_Damage` once per object, which gives `AffectsAir`, `AffectsGround` and `DamageAirThreshold` a single place.
- `TechnoTypeClass::Compute_CRC` and `Serialize` are the two places every type field goes through.

Build order, smallest first:

1. Parse, serialise and document all keys (the common work above), unused at first.
2. Cursor and selection keys: `NoManualEnter`, `NoSelfGuardArea`, `NoManualFire`, `NoManualMove`, `NoManualUnload`, `MassSelectable`.
3. Feedback keys: `DieSound.Override`, `VoiceDie.Override`, `UnitLost.Suppress` (add the warhead argument to `Death_Announcement`), `Malicious`, `Flash.Duration`, `CloakSound`, `DecloakSound`.
4. Damage-path switches in `TechnoClass::Take_Damage` and `InfantryClass::Take_Damage`: the house filter (`AffectsOwner`, `AffectsEnemies`, `AffectsNeutral`), `AffectsBelowPercent`, `AffectsAbovePercent`, `AffectsVeterancy`, `PreventScatter`, `DeathWeapon.*`, `Damage.Deployed`, `CanKill`, `PreventCrewEscape`, `PreventPassengerEscape`, `PreventOccupantEscape`.
5. `Explosion_Damage`: `DamageAirThreshold`, `AffectsAir`, `AffectsGround`.
6. Per-type values: `Berserk.ROFMultiplier`, `ImmuneToBerserk`, `IronCurtain.Modifier`, `ForceShield.Modifier`, `Cloakable.Stages`, `Cloakable.Allowed`, `TurretROT`, `UseDisguiseMovementSpeed`, `CylinderRangefinding`.
7. Targeting, cargo and economy: `CanPassiveAquire.Guard` and `.Cloak` (after Open questions 3 is settled), `CivilianEnemy`, `OmniCrusher.Aggressive`, `Passengers.*`, `Prerequisite.Negative`, `IgnoreForBaseCenter`, `NoRearm.*`.

## Saved state

- Every key is a type, warhead, weapon or rules field. Serialise each in the `Serialize` method of its class; the save revision rises once for the whole group.
- No new per-object, per-house or global runtime state. `CanKill`, `PreventCrewEscape` and the other warhead keys act within one call to `Take_Damage`. `BuildingClass::IsSurvivorless` is saved already and checksummed already.
- Add to the rules checksum (`Compute_CRC`) the fields that change game state, so that two machines with different rules files are caught: `Cloakable.Allowed`, `Cloakable.Stages`, `TurretROT`, `ImmuneToBerserk`, `Berserk.ROFMultiplier`, `IronCurtain.Modifier`, `ForceShield.Modifier`, `CivilianEnemy`, `CanPassiveAquire.*`, `OmniCrusher.Aggressive`, `Passengers.*`, `Prerequisite.Negative`, `UseDisguiseMovementSpeed`, `NoRearm.*`, `CylinderRangefinding`, `IgnoreForBaseCenter` and every warhead key except `DieSound.Override`, `VoiceDie.Override`, `UnitLost.Suppress`, `Malicious` and `Flash.Duration`. Those five, the two cloak sounds, `MassSelectable` and the `NoManual*` keys change only what one player hears, sees or sends and are left out. The existing checksum is not exhaustive (`AffectsAllies` is not in it), so the owner decides how far to go (Open questions 13).
- Determinism: no key uses a random number or the frame count except through code that already does. `Berserk.ROFMultiplier` uses a float multiply and a truncation; use the same type on every machine. The `DieSound` and `VoiceDie` picks use the unsynchronised generator and stay that way.

## Open questions

1. Order of the new Phobos filters (`AffectsNeutral`, `AffectsBelowPercent`, `AffectsAbovePercent`, `AffectsVeterancy`) against the Iron Curtain and warping tests in `TechnoClass::Take_Damage`. Phobos runs them first. Put them next to the existing `AffectsAllies` test, which also runs before the Iron Curtain test.
2. `CivilianEnemy`: the Ares page says the object is "not considered neutral in multiplayer matches". The engine has two tests (`IsMultiplayPassive`, then `IsInsignificant`). Decide whether the key lifts one or both, and whether it matters in single-player.
3. `CanPassiveAquire.Cloak`: the Ares page gives the default `yes`, meaning a cloak-capable human object on Guard acquires targets. The manual says stock YR holds fire in that case. Either Ares changes the stock behaviour or the page describes a Tiberian Sun default. Test 4 settles it; build step 7 waits for the result.
4. `NoManualFire`: the Ares page says "target selection" and "attack cursor". Whether the control+alt guard-area click and the force-move click are also refused is not stated. Test 1 records it.
5. `Cloakable.Allowed=no` and attached-effect cloaking, and whether a unit already cloaked when its type is converted by a script or a Phobos `Convert` keeps its cloak.
6. `Passengers.Allowed` and `Passengers.Disallowed` with the same type in both lists. The page does not say which wins; refusing is the safe reading.
7. `Prerequisite.Negative` for computer houses, and whether `Is_Secret_Item` and reverse-engineered types obey it. The page says only that `PrerequisiteOverride` does not bypass it.
8. `NoManualMove` on a building with `UndeploysInto` and a factory role: does the move click still set the rally point? Phobos source skips a block of `BuildingClass::CellClickedAction` that the engine does not map one to one. Test with the real Phobos build.
9. Duration unit for `ForceShield.Modifier` (the page says seconds), and rounding for both modifiers. Assume frames and truncation toward zero.
10. `CylinderRangefinding`: the Phobos docs say airborne technos always ignore height, the code says aircraft. The engine ignores height for any airborne shooter. Keep the engine rule unless a test with a jump-jet shows otherwise.
11. `AffectsAir` and `AffectsGround`: the Phobos docs say 208 leptons, the code uses the object's airborne state. Use the engine's `In_Air()`; confirm that hovering jump-jets and landed aircraft sort the way Phobos sorts them.
12. `UnitLost.Suppress` for structures, and for the `RADAREVENT_UNIT_LOST` radar event that plays with the line.
13. How much of the new data goes into `Compute_CRC`. Adding fields makes mods with different rules desync-check differently; the project may prefer a single hash of the INI instead.
14. Mental Omega usage. All keys were picked without counts. Run `mod_scan.py` on the mod and drop any key the scan does not find.

## Test plan

Use a skirmish map and the map editor. Write the INI below into `rulesmd.ini` of a test mod. Tests marked Ares need Ares 3.0 with `Ares.dll`; tests marked Phobos need a Phobos build on top of Ares 3.0. Run each test first in the real game to record the result, then again in OpenYR once the key is built. Names such as `MYAPC` are copies of existing sections with the new `Name` and `UIName`; `E1` is the GI, `MTNK` the Grizzly, `HTNK` the Rhino.

1. Orders (Ares, Phobos). `[MTNK] NoManualFire=yes` and `NoManualMove=yes` (second part Phobos). Select the Grizzly and hover over an enemy unit in range: the cursor stays the select cursor and a right-click does nothing. Move an enemy unit into range: the Grizzly fires on its own. Shoot the Grizzly from out of its range: it fires back. Hold the control key over the enemy: still no attack cursor. Right-click the ground: no move cursor, no movement. Edge: select a Grizzly and a GI together and right-click the ground: only the GI moves. Add `[MYAPC] NoManualUnload=yes NoManualEnter=yes`, load three GIs through a map trigger, select the transport and press the deploy key: nothing unloads and hovering over it shows no deploy cursor. Hover a GI over the transport: select cursor, not enter. Edge: a team script that loads GIs into the `NoManualEnter` transport still succeeds.
2. Healer cursor (Ares). Copy `E1` as `[MYHEAL]`, give it a `Primary` weapon whose warhead has negative damage (copy the Medic's weapon) and `Deployer=yes`, and add `NoSelfGuardArea=yes`. Hover over the healer: the deploy cursor shows. Without the key the guard-area cursor shows.
3. Civilians (Ares). In a skirmish map, place a Neutral-owned armed `E1` (a copy named `[MYCIV]` with `CivilianEnemy=yes`) and an identical one without the key. Put an owned Grizzly on Guard 3 cells from each. The Grizzly fires at the first and ignores the second. Edge: repeat with `Insignificant=yes` on the first and record whether it is still shot (Open questions 2).
4. Guard-mode scanning (Ares). `[MTNK] CanPassiveAquire.Guard=no`. Put it on Guard; walk an enemy GI into range: no fire. Switch to Guard area with control+alt click: it fires. Next, `[MTNK] Cloakable=yes` with `CanPassiveAquire.Guard=yes` and `CanPassiveAquire.Cloak=no`, then with `CanPassiveAquire.Cloak=yes` (the default): record whether the cloaking Grizzly fires on Guard in each case. This is the stock-versus-Ares comparison for Open questions 3. Edge: `Guard=no` and `Cloak=yes` together: no fire.
5. Omni crusher (Ares). `[HTNK] OmniCrusher=yes OmniCrusher.Aggressive=no`. Order an attack on an enemy tank 10 cells away: the Rhino stops at weapon range and shoots. With the key `yes` it drives up and crushes. Edge: a move order through a group of enemy infantry still crushes them.
6. Cargo (Ares). `[MYAPC] Passengers=5 SizeLimit=2`, `[E1] Size=2`. With `Passengers.BySize` unset: two GIs board, a third gets the no-enter cursor. With `[MYAPC] Passengers.BySize=no`: five board. Then `Passengers.Disallowed=E1`: no GI boards; `Passengers.Allowed=E2`: Conscripts board and GIs do not. Edge: put `E1` in both lists and record the outcome (Open questions 6).
7. Prerequisites (Ares). `[E1] Prerequisite.Negative=GAAIRC`. Build the Air Force Command: the GI icon leaves the sidebar. Sell it: the icon returns. Edge: add `PrerequisiteOverride=GAAIRC` to the same GI; the GI icon still stays hidden while the building stands.
8. Cloak (Ares). `[MTNK] Cloakable=yes Cloakable.Allowed=no`: the Grizzly never cloaks, including inside a Yuri cloak generator field. Place a cloak crate and drive a Grizzly with the key onto it: the player gets money. Without the key the crate cloaks it. Then `CloakSound=<soundmd entry A>`, `DecloakSound=<entry B>`: entry A plays at cloaking, entry B at decloaking. With `Cloakable.Stages=3` against the default 9 and `CloakingSpeed=5`, the fade finishes in about a third of the time (15 frames against 45).
9. Berserk and Iron Curtain (Ares). Copy the Chaos Drone weapon with a `Psychedelic=yes` warhead. A GI with `ImmuneToPsionics=yes ImmuneToBerserk=no` goes berserk; a Grizzly with `ImmuneToBerserk=yes` does not. With a berserk Grizzly (`ROF=60`) and `Berserk.ROFMultiplier=0.25`, count shots over 300 frames: 20, against 10 at the default 0.5. Set `[General] IronCurtainDuration=600`, `[MTNK] IronCurtain.Modifier=0.5`: the Iron Curtain on a Grizzly ends after about 300 frames. With `IronCurtain.Modifier=0` the Grizzly is not curtained. `[GAPOWR] ForceShield.Modifier=0`: a Force Shield does not cover the power plant.
10. Turret speed (Ares). `[MTNK] ROT=5 TurretROT=20`: order an attack at a target behind the tank; the turret reaches it in about a quarter of the frames the body would need for the same turn. With no `TurretROT`, the turret turns at the body's `ROT`.
11. Warhead filters (Ares, Phobos). Copy `[AP]`'s weapon with `CellSpread=3`, `Damage=50` and fire it at the middle of a group: one enemy Grizzly, one owned Grizzly, one allied Grizzly, one Neutral GI, one Kirov airship in the air. Use a new warhead per row: (a) `AffectsEnemies=no`: only owned and allied units are hurt. (b) `AffectsAllies=no AffectsOwner=yes`: only the owned and enemy units are hurt. (c) `AffectsNeutral=no`: the Neutral GI stays whole. (d) `AffectsBelowPercent=0.5`: a full-health enemy Grizzly stays whole; the same Grizzly at 50% is hurt. (e) `AffectsVeterancy=elite`: only an elite Grizzly is hurt. (f) `AffectsAir=no`: the Kirov is not hurt. (g) `AffectsGround=no`: only the Kirov is hurt. Edge: `AffectsBelowPercent=0.5` with forced damage from a trigger action; Phobos still ignores the damage.
12. Air threshold (Ares). A warhead with `CellSpread=3`, force-fired at the ground below a Kirov in flight: no damage at the default. With `DamageAirThreshold=-1` the Kirov is hurt. With `DamageAirThreshold=300` and a ground blast: no damage.
13. Cannot kill (Phobos). Warhead `CanKill=no`, damage 1000, fired at a Grizzly (`Strength=300`): it ends at 1 hit point. A second shot leaves it at 1. Edge: a warhead with negative damage heals as usual.
14. Escapes (Phobos). Set `[General] CrewEscape=100%`. A Grizzly killed by a stock warhead releases a crew soldier; killed by one with `PreventCrewEscape=yes` it releases none. A `MYAPC` with three GIs: stock warhead, the GIs step out; `PreventPassengerEscape=yes`, they die. A civilian building garrisoned by three GIs: `PreventOccupantEscape=yes` kills the GIs with the building.
15. Hit feedback (Ares). Kill a GI with a warhead using `DieSound.Override=<none>`: no death scream. Kill an owned Grizzly with `UnitLost.Suppress=yes`: no "unit lost" line. Hit an owned harvester with `Malicious=no`: no "ore miner under attack" line. With `Flash.Duration=30`: the target flashes for about 30 frames. `DeathWeapon.SuppressInfantry=yes`: a GI with `Explodes=yes` and a visible `DeathWeapon` dies without the blast; a Grizzly with the same settings still blasts. Edge: `DeathWeapon.Suppress=E1` and `SuppressInfantry=no` gives the same result for the GI only. A deployed Guardian GI hit by `Damage.Deployed=0.5` loses half the damage a standing GI loses.
16. Phobos misc. `[E1] UseDisguiseMovementSpeed=yes` on a Spy disguised as a GI with `Speed=1` set on `E1`: the Spy crawls while disguised and moves at its own speed after the disguise is lost. `[MTNK] NoRearm.UnderEMP=yes ROF=300`: fire once, then hit the Grizzly with an Ares EMP warhead (`EMP.Duration=200`) about 10 frames later; the next shot comes about 500 frames after the first. Without the key it comes at 300. `IgnoreForBaseCenter=yes` on the MCV type, with the start MCV far east of the other starting units: the first view centres on the other units. `CylinderRangefinding=yes` on the weapon section of a tower on a cliff three levels above a target 5 cells away, weapon `Range=5.5`: it fires; without the key the height difference puts the target out of range.
