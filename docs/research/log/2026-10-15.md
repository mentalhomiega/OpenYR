## Open points from the specs

- Ares `CanPassiveAquire.Cloak` defaults to yes in the Ares documentation, but the manual says stock Yuri's Revenge already holds fire for cloak-capable human units on Guard. Test 4 of the small-switches spec settles it.
- Phobos `AffectsAir` and `AffectsGround` disagree between its documentation (208 leptons) and its code (the airborne state). `CylinderRangefinding` is documented for any airborne techno and coded for aircraft only. The engine has no neutral-house test for `AffectsNeutral`.
- The Phobos lead-time maths for Straight and Bombard trajectories under-leads receding targets. Phobos trajectory maths uses the C library trig, so Win32 and x64 builds could diverge; the library choice is yours.
- Whether a `Parachuted` projectile detonates on landing without `Dropping=yes` needs a check in the game.
- The Ares superweapon spec leaves open how Ares resolves two superweapons that share an `Action=`, whether upgrades count for `SW.AuxBuildings`, and whether the lightning `SW.Range` is a radius or a diameter. Phobos shows likely bugs in its inhibitor and designator loops, `SW.Next` has no cycle guard, and the EMPulse un-suspend index looks wrong; the spec leaves matching them to you.
- `FreeMCV` is read but ignored by the engine, so its tick marks a key that does nothing. Whether a tick should require an effect is undecided.
